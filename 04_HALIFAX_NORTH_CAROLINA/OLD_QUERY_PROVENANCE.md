# 1997 Halifax Query — Provenance and Limits

The December 1997 NCGenWeb query is signed **Jim Partin** and asks for the parents, birthplace, marriage date and Mary's maiden name of John Partin.

It reports:
- John b. ca 1722, d. Jul 1795 Halifax;
- Mary b. ca 1740, d. ca 1805;
- children Robert, Brasel, Barnabas, David, Charles, Lucy, Sarah, Mary, Priscilla, Elizabeth;
- 100 acres adjoining Great Darlling Swamp bought from William and Ann Hux, 11 Aug 1767.

## Crucial inference
Because the submitter was explicitly **seeking John's parents and Mary's maiden name**, the query itself demonstrates those identities were unresolved in that research tradition in 1997.

Therefore later online trees assigning precise parents or a maiden name to Mary must be independently sourced; they cannot be back-projected into this query.

Grade: **C+ lead set**, pending original records.

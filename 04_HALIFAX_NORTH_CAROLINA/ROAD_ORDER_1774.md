# 1774 Halifax Road Order — Community Reconstruction Target

## Existing anchor
John Partin is reported in a 1774 Halifax road appointment/order.

## Why road records matter
Road orders often name clusters of nearby householders required to maintain a defined section of road. The complete entry can therefore reconstruct John's immediate neighborhood even when deeds are sparse.

## Extraction protocol
Capture:
- date;
- road name/route;
- overseer;
- every named worker/householder;
- landmarks, ferries, bridges, creeks;
- prior and subsequent road orders.

Then compare those names with the 1767 Hux deed, 1772 Fort deed, tax lists and 1795 probate.

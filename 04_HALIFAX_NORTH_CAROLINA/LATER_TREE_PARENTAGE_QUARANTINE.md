# Later Tree Parentage — Quarantine

Modern Ancestry aggregation pages assign the Halifax-style John family to specific parents such as William Cocke Partin and Johannah/Hannah Stith.

## Conflict
The older 1997 Halifax researcher who supplied the John/Barnabas/Brasel family structure explicitly asked for John's parents and Mary's maiden name.

## Rule
Later precise parent assignments are **not inherited evidence**. Require:
- probate naming John;
- deed of gift;
- guardianship;
- Bible/church record;
- or a coherent land/tax migration chain plus corroboration.

Until then, keep John's parents **unknown** in the proved tree.

# 1795 John Partin Will — Extraction Protocol

The will is the key endpoint for the Halifax generation.

## Do not rely on family-tree summaries
The original or high-quality image must be transcribed clause by clause.

## Extract separately
- date written;
- date proved;
- residence;
- wife;
- every beneficiary and exact relationship term;
- executors;
- witnesses;
- land descriptions;
- enslaved persons if present;
- livestock/tools/household goods;
- debts/notes;
- residual clause;
- signatures/marks.

## Probate packet
Also seek inventory, sale, executor bond, accounts, distributions, guardianships and later estate litigation.

## Genealogical objective
Establish exactly how Robert, Charles, Barnabas, David and other reported children are described. Do not infer relationships from name lists when the wording can be recovered.

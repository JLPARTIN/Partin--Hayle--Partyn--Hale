# Hux Migration Test — Surry to Halifax

## Virginia seed
A 1737 Surry abstract places **William Partin** and **Elizabeth Hux** together as witnesses.

## North Carolina seed
Older research reports an **11 Aug 1767** Halifax conveyance from **William and Ann Hux** to **John Partin**, 100 acres adjoining Great Darlling Swamp.

## Why this is potentially significant
An uncommon associate surname occurring with Partins in both the Virginia and Halifax environments can be more discriminating than the Partin surname alone.

## Required proof chain
1. Identify Elizabeth Hux in 1737.
2. Reconstruct her household/parents/siblings.
3. Identify William Hux and wife Ann in Halifax.
4. Determine whether William is kin to Elizabeth.
5. Trace the Hux migration independently of the Partins.
6. Compare witnesses/neighbors in the 1767 deed.

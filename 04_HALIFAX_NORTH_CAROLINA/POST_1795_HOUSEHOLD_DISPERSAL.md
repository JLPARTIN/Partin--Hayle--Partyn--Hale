# Post-1795 Halifax Household Dispersal

## Documented first foothold
David Partin appears in the 1797 Halifax insolvent-tax list, one free poll, District No.2.

## Research model
If the 1795 probate identifies David, Barnabas, Brasil, Robert and Charles as sons/heirs, their locations from 1795–1810 can reveal whether the estate household fragmented into:
- Halifax stayers;
- South Carolina migrants;
- Mississippi migrants.

## Required searches
- Halifax tax lists 1795–1810 for all Partin variants.
- Deeds disposing of inherited John Partin land.
- Estate distributions/guardian accounts.
- South Carolina census/tax/land records for Brasil/Barnabas.
- Mississippi territorial records for Barnabas.

## Key warning
Do not assume every Partin in the 1790s Halifax indexes is John's child. Use district, land and probate relationships.

# Halifax in the 1790s — Multiple Partin Household Warning

Search-index evidence surfaces several Partin names in Halifax-era compiled census/substitute material, including John, William and Benjamin, while David is independently present in the 1797 insolvent-tax list.

## Consequence
The late-eighteenth-century Halifax Partin population cannot safely be modeled as a single household without primary enumeration/tax context.

## Required work
Separate:
- John Partin entries;
- William Partin;
- Benjamin Partin;
- David Partin;
- any Robert/Charles/Barnabas/Brasil entries.

For each, capture district, neighbors, poll/acreage, date and record type.

## Warning
A “John Partin” index hit after 1795 could be a different John and cannot automatically contradict or extend the reported July 1795 death.

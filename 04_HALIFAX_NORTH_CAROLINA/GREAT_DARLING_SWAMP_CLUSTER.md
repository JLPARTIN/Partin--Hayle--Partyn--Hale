# Great Darling Swamp Cluster

## Geographic anchor
The reported 1767 Hux→John Partin tract adjoined Great Darlling/Darling Swamp.

## Research strategy
Reconstruct every adjoining owner and nearby deed for a ten-year window around 1767. Search both grantor and witness networks rather than only Partin entries.

## Questions
- Was John already occupying the land?
- Did Hux own neighboring acreage?
- Did Fort own nearby land?
- Are any Virginia FAN surnames present?
- Does the 1774 road order traverse this neighborhood?
- Does John's 1795 will dispose of the same tract?

## Value
Land continuity from 1767 to 1795 can prove that the scattered Halifax records refer to one John even if no record states his age.

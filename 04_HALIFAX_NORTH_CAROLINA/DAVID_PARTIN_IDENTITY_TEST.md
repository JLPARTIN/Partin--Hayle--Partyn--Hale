# David Partin — Identity Test

## Hypothesis
He is the David reported as a child/heir of John Partin d.1795.

## Tests

- purchase at estate sale;
- inherited land;
- same district/neighbors as John's tract;
- later deed reciting inheritance;
- sibling co-occurrence.

## Alternative

Status: **documented person; relationship unresolved.**

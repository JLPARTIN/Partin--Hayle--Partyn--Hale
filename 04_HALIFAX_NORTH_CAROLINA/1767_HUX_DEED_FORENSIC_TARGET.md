# 11 August 1767 Hux → John Partin Deed — Forensic Target

## Reported record
Older Halifax research reports William Hux and wife Ann conveyed to John Partin **100 acres adjoining Great Darlling Swamp** on 11 Aug 1767.

## Why this is now a decisive record
The Hux surname already appears beside a Partin in Surry: William Partin and Elizabeth Hux are reported as witnesses in 1737. The 1767 deed can therefore test whether an associate network crossed the Virginia–North Carolina boundary.

## Full extraction requirements
Do not stop with grantor/grantee names. Capture:
- county and deed book/page;
- consideration;
- John's stated residence;
- full metes/bounds;
- adjoining owners;
- witnesses;
- Ann Hux's dower/acknowledgment;
- recording date;
- prior title chain.

## Best possible result
Language identifying John as “of” a Virginia county would be direct migration evidence. Short of that, recurring witnesses/neighbors can still provide a network bridge.

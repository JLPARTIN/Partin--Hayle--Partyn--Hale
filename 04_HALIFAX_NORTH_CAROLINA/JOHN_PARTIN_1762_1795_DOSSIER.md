# John Partin — Halifax/Edgecombe Dossier, 1762–1795

## Current anchors requiring original-image upgrades
- 1762 tax entry.
- 11 Aug 1767 reported Hux→John Partin deed, 100 acres adjoining Great Darlling Swamp.
- 1772 Edgecombe deed involving Elias Fort.
- 1774 Halifax road appointment.
- 1782 tax.
- 1795 Halifax will/probate.

## Research objective
Treat these as one man's longitudinal record only after identity continuity is tested through location, associates, land and chronology.

## 1767 deed significance
The reported grantors William and Ann Hux are especially important because Hux appears with William Partin in a 1737 Surry record. If the Hux families can be connected, that would be independent FAN evidence supporting Virginia→Halifax migration.

## 1795 endpoint
The will reportedly names Mary, Robert, Charles, Barnabas and David among the family. The complete will/probate must be recovered to distinguish sons, daughters, spouses, executors and witnesses precisely.

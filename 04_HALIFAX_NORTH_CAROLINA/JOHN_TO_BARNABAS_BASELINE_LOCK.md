# John → Barnabas Baseline Lock

The December 1997 Jim Partin query remains a useful hypothesis baseline:
- John d. Jul 1795 Halifax;
- wife Mary;
- Barnabas b. 29 Aug 1773, d. 24 Mar 1855 Wilkinson MS;
- Brasel to Sumter/Kershaw;
- David and Charles also named.

## What is locked
This is the **reported reconstruction**, not a proved pedigree.

## What must independently verify it
1. 1795 probate naming Barnabas or equivalent heir evidence.
2. post-1795 Halifax record linking Barnabas to inherited/family property.
3. SC records showing the same Barnabas.
4. MS records showing the same man.
5. DNA documentary chain to MK85913's entered ancestor.

No modern tree can substitute for these links.

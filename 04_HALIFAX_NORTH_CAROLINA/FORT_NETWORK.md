# Fort–Partin Network — Halifax / Edgecombe

## Anchor
The working chronology contains a 1772 Edgecombe deed involving John Partin and Elias Fort.

## Why Fort matters
Elias Fort may provide another independent associate for distinguishing the Halifax John and reconstructing his neighborhood.

## Research tasks
- recover full 1772 deed;
- determine grantor/grantee roles;
- map land;
- identify witnesses;
- reconstruct Elias Fort's Virginia/NC origins;
- test whether Fort appears with Partins before Halifax;
- follow Fort probate and family deeds for Partin references.

## Caution
Do not convert repeated neighborhood contact into kinship without evidence.

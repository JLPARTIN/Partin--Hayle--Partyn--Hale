# John Partin 1795 Family — Claim Audit

## Older research claim
The 1997 Halifax NCGenWeb query reports John Partin, died Jul 1795, wife Mary, and children:
Robert; Brasel; Barnabas; David; Charles; Lucy; Sarah; Mary; Priscilla; Elizabeth.

It further assigns Barnabas a birth of 29 Aug 1773 and later death in Wilkinson County, Mississippi.

## What this means
This is useful as a search inventory, **not proof of parent-child relationships**. The will/probate must establish the exact wording.

## Conflicting internet pedigrees
Current web trees give materially inconsistent parents, birth years and spouses for the same supposed John. That disagreement is evidence that tree consensus cannot resolve his identity.

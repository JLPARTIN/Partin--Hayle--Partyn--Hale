# John Partin & Winifred — Halifax to Kershaw Dossier

## 1793 Halifax anchor
A Halifax deed records John Partin and wife Winifred conveying 100 acres on March Swamp to William Dickens.

Recovered locator:
- FamilySearch Image Group 007537923;
- image 565/811;
- manuscript p.500 in one recovered note;
- acknowledged 20 Feb 1793;
- registered 10 Apr 1793;
- witnesses Mary Dickens, Joseph Mark Dickens, Jesse Dickens;
- Winifred relinquished dower before Willis Alston.

A separate older note labels the deed Book 17 p.97 / 30 Jan 1793. This locator/date discrepancy must be reconciled from the image rather than silently normalized.

## South Carolina identity
By 1800–1802, John Partin/Parton held a 150-acre tract on Pine Tree Creek in Kershaw District.

On 24 Feb 1802 he conveyed the tract. Winifred relinquished dower on 4 Mar 1802 before J.P. William Lang/Langley.

Recovered notes disagree on the grantee surname (De Bruhl vs Drake) and witness reading (M./Waughan/Vaughan). The deed image must settle these readings.

## Identity assessment
The repeated John + Winifred marital pair across Halifax and Kershaw is **STRONG** evidence of the same couple, especially because dower relinquishment independently identifies the wife in both jurisdictions.

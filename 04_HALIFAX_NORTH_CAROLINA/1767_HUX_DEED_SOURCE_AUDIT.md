# 1767 Hux → John Partin — Source Audit

## What web recovery actually establishes
A 1997 Halifax NCGenWeb query by Jim Partin reports that John Partin owned 100 acres adjoining **Great Darlling Swamp**, purchased from **William and Ann Hux on 11 Aug 1767**.

This is a valuable old research lead but **not a deed transcription** and supplies no deed-book/page citation.

## Important correction
Until the deed itself is recovered, do not label the residence, acreage, grantor identities or spelling of the swamp as primary-record proven merely because the query is specific.

## Source
https://www.ncgenweb.us/halifax/queries/queryarch1997.htm

## Retrieval target
Search Halifax deed indexes around 1767 under:
Partin / Parton / Partain / Parten
and
Hux / Hucks / Huxx.

Once found, capture book/page, consideration, witnesses, metes/bounds, prior title and Ann's acknowledgment.

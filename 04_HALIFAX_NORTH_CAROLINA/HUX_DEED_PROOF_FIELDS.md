# 1767 Hux → John Partin — Proof Fields

The 1997 query reports William and Ann Hux sold John Partin 100 acres adjoining Great Darlling Swamp on 11 Aug 1767.

## Original deed must answer
- county/book/page;
- grantor residence;
- grantee residence;
- consideration;
- exact swamp spelling;
- metes/bounds;
- neighbors;
- prior patent/deed;
- witnesses;
- Ann's role and acknowledgment;
- recording date.

## Why prior title matters
Tracing William Hux backward may reveal whether the Hux–Partin association in Halifax is independent of, or continuous with, the earlier Surry William Partin / Elizabeth Hux witness network.

No kinship should be inferred from surname recurrence alone.

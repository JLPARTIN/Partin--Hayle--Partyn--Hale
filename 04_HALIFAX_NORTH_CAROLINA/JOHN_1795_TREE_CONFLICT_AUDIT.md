# John Partin — 1795 Internet Tree Conflict Audit

Modern public-tree aggregations disagree materially about the Halifax-era John.

Examples include:
- a John born about 1745 with different children;
- a John attached to Mary Hewitt;
- a John assigned to unrelated North Carolina Partin parents;
- the older Halifax query's John born about 1722 with wife Mary unknown.

## Research value
These contradictions are not evidence for any one pedigree. They demonstrate why exact probate, deed and tax records must control.

## Quarantine rule
Do not import:
- exact birth year;
- Mary's maiden name;
- named parents;
from public trees unless supported by a cited primary record.

## Search utility
Tree claims may supply candidate spellings, spouses and places to test, but never close an identity question.

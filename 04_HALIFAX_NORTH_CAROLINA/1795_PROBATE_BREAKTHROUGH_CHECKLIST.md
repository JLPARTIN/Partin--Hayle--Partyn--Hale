# 1795 John Partin Probate — Breakthrough Checklist

This is now the single most valuable Halifax record set.

## First pass
Transcribe every relationship word exactly.

## Second pass
Build four lists:
1. beneficiaries;
2. executors/witnesses;
3. securities/appraisers;
4. estate purchasers/creditors.

## Third pass
Search each list against 1762–1797 deeds, taxes and road orders.

## Questions capable of being settled
- Is Mary wife/widow?
- Are Barnabas, David, Brasil/Brasel, Robert and Charles explicitly sons?
- Which daughters are named and with what married surnames?
- Is Great Darling Swamp land devised?
- Does Hux appear anywhere in the packet?
- Is David's 1797 district consistent with inherited land?

## Capture
Will, probate order, executor bond, inventory, sale, accounts, distributions and guardianships are separate evidentiary objects. Do not stop after finding the will.

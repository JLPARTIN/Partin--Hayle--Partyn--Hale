# Halifax / Edgecombe Record Targets — John Partin Cluster

## Current state
The repository preserves a later-eighteenth-century John Partin cluster, but the original images/citations need systematic recovery.

An archived 1997 NCGenWeb query by Jim Partin gives an older family reconstruction: John Partin, born about 1722 and died July 1795 Halifax County; wife Mary; children including Robert, Brasel, Barnabas, David, Charles, Lucy, Sarah, Mary, Priscilla and Elizabeth. It also claims John purchased **100 acres adjoining Great Darlling Swamp from William and Ann Hux on 11 August 1767**.

This is a lead, not proof.

## Priority recovery sequence
1. Halifax deed for 11 Aug 1767, William and Ann Hux → John Partin, 100 acres adjoining Great Darlling Swamp.
2. 1762 tax entry attributed to John.
3. 1772 Edgecombe deed involving Elias Fort.
4. 1774 Halifax road appointment.
5. 1782 tax entry.
6. 1795 Halifax will and complete probate packet.

## Why the 1767 deed matters
If recovered, it may provide the earliest tightly localized land anchor for the Halifax John and identify neighbors/witnesses capable of linking him backward toward Virginia.

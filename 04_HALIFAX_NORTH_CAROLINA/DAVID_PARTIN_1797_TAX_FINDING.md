# David Partin — 1797 Halifax Tax Finding

## New record
Halifax County court insolvent-tax abstracts for the August 1798 court include the 1797 collection list for District No.2, collector **William Read**, and name:

**DAVID PARTIN — 1 free poll**

## Significance
The 1997 family reconstruction listed David among the children of the John Partin who died in 1795. This 1797 tax record independently places an adult David Partin in Halifax immediately after John's death.

It does **not** by itself prove David was John's son, but it substantially improves David from a tree/query name to a documented local adult.

## Neighborhood clue
The same district includes **William Faucett — 1 free poll**. District reconstruction may identify the Partin neighborhood after John's death.

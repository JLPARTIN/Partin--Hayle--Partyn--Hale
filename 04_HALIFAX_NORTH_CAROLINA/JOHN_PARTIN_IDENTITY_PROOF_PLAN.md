# John Partin — Identity Proof Plan

## Proposition
The John Partin documented in Halifax/Edgecombe from the 1760s through 1795 is the same individual across tax, deed, road and probate records.

## Proof dimensions
1. **Geography** — same tract/watershed/neighbors.
2. **FAN network** — Hux, Fort and recurring witnesses.
3. **Chronology** — no conflicting simultaneous John households.
4. **Property continuity** — acquisition, taxation, road duty, devise.
5. **Family continuity** — names in probate match earlier deeds/taxes.
6. **Virginia bridge** — evidence linking this man backward.

## Separate proposition
Even after the Halifax John is internally proved, his identity with the Virginia John remains a separate question. Do not collapse the two proof problems.

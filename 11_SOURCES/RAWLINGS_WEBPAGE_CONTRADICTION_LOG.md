# Rawlings Webpage — Contradiction Log

Source: Descendants of Gregory Rawlings, Oocities mirror.

## Contradiction A
John III chronology:
“one to his brother in law (I THINK) CHARLES PARTIN”

## Contradiction B
Elizabeth notes:
“May be Robert Partin who bought John's land in 1706”
then:
“one to his brother in law (I THINK) ROBERT PARTIN”

## Concrete citation supplied
Only the Robert version is followed by:
Surry Co Deeds No.5 (1694–1709) p.348 — John Rawlings→Robert Partin, 200 acres, Blackwater, patent 29 Apr 1692, recorded 7 May 1706.

## Later ambiguity
The page says perhaps John oversaw land “for Robert or Charles Partin.”

## Use rule
The page is valuable as a **finding aid**, but its narrative reconstruction must not be treated as internally consistent evidence.

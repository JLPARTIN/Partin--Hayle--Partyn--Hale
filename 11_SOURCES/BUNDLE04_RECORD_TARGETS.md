# Bundle 04 — Exact Record Targets

## A. 13 Apr 1642 Partin → Llewellyn conveyance
Highest priority. Find the deed and patent abstract that reportedly states Robert Partin the younger was the son of Robert Partin the elder.

## B. 15 Apr 1648 John Seward patent
Reported 1,200 acres, Isle of Wight, branch of Roanoke called Blackwater; headrights include Robert, Margret, Robert Jr., Avis, Rebecca and Debora Partin.

## C. 9 Aug 1657 Francis Hooke patent
Reported 100 acres, Elizabeth City County, land in tenure/occupation of Robert Partin and Gresham Covell.

## D. 1676–1688 probate sweep
The previously inherited “1677 inventory” remains unverified. Search Charles City, Henrico, Elizabeth City, Isle of Wight and Surry court/probate material for Partin variants. Also test the unsourced internet claim of a 14 Jun 1687 death.

## E. 1692 Rawlings patent
John/Jonathan Rawlings patent dated 29 Apr 1692, later described in the 1706 conveyance. Recover patent and map bounds.

## F. 1706 deed
Surry Deeds No. 5, p. 348. Recover image and determine whether any relationship between Rawlings and Partin is stated or merely assumed by later genealogists.

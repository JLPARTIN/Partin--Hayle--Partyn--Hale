# Bundle 14 — Source Register

## Rawlings/Charles
**Descendants of Gregory Rawlings** — derivative compilation with quoted/transcribed Surry records and author commentary. It explicitly describes one 1706 Rawlings land piece as sold to “his brother in law Charles Partin,” and reports Charles's 1727 “late in the tenure of John Rawlings” language.
https://www.oocities.org/colosseum/park/2693/gregory15941653.html

**John Rawlings 1720–1752 research page** — repeats the 1706 two-piece model and Charles brother-in-law claim; includes 1739 Surry Book 9 p.119 boundary citation.
https://www.oocities.org/colosseum/park/2693/john17201752.html

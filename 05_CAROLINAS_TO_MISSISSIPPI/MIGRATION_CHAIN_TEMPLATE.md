# Carolinas → Mississippi Migration Chain

## Stage 1 — Halifax
Anchor Barnabas to John's probate and reconstruct inherited/occupied property.

## Stage 2 — North Carolina departure
Find final tax/deed/court appearance and any sale preceding migration.

## Stage 3 — South Carolina
Identify first district record, land, tax/census household and associates.

## Stage 4 — Mississippi
Identify first record and compare household/FAN network to South Carolina.

## Evidence standard
A migration link becomes STRONG when at least two independent dimensions agree, e.g. name + associates, land sale + acquisition, household composition + chronology.

## Goal
Produce a generation-by-generation documentary chain, not a state-to-state family-tree arrow.

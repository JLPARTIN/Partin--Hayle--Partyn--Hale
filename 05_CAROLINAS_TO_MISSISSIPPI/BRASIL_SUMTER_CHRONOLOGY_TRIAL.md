# Brasil/Brasel → Sumter Chronology Trial

The 1997 Halifax query places Brasel Partin:
- born ca 1770;
- died ca 1835;
- Sumter/Kershaw County, South Carolina.

A modern FamilySearch profile places **Asa Brasil Partin** as father of Henry Washington Partin, born in Sumter in 1818.

## Testable hypothesis
The distinctive Brasil name may have passed from the older Brasel/Brasil to Asa Brasil.

## Chronology
If Brasel was born c.1770, he is old enough to be Asa's father or uncle. This is chronologically possible but not proof.

## Records to target
- 1790–1840 census entries for Part*n in Camden/Kershaw/Sumter;
- Sumter/Kershaw deeds;
- probate around 1835;
- Asa's marriage and earliest land record;
- estate buyers/witnesses;
- guardianship if Asa was young when older Brasil died.

## Goal
Prove the relationship with a record, not the repeated name.

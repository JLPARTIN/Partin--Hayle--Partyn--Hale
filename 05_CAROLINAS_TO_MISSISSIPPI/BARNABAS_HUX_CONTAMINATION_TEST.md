# Barnabas × Margaret/Peggy Hux — Contamination Test

The repeated modern claim that Barnabas married Margaret/Peggy Hux is potentially important but may also reflect pedigree copying.

## Test for independence
A source counts as independent only if it derives the relationship from a record rather than another online tree.

### Strong evidence
- marriage bond/license;
- deed naming Barnabas and wife Margaret;
- Hux probate naming daughter Margaret Partin;
- Partin probate naming wife Margaret;
- child record naming both parents.

### Weak/non-independent evidence
- Ancestry public tree profile;
- copied FamilySearch relationship;
- unsourced genealogy page;
- Find a Grave biography copied from a tree.

## Reason for caution
Because Hux already appears in the known Partin research, later tree builders may have attached a Hux wife to Barnabas from the same tradition. We must rule out circular propagation before treating the three-generation Hux pattern as real.

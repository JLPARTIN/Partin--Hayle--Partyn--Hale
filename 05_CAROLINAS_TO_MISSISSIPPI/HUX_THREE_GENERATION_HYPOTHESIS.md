# Hux Three-Generation Hypothesis

## Observed/reported pattern
1. **1737 Surry:** William Partin and Elizabeth Hux occur together as witnesses.
2. **1767 Halifax lead:** William & Ann Hux reportedly convey land to John Partin.
3. **Barnabas generation:** modern pedigrees repeatedly identify Barnabas's wife as Margaret/Peggy Hux.

## Competing explanations
A. One extended Hux network remained associated with the Partins across migration and marriage.
B. The Hux events involve unrelated households sharing the surname.
C. One or more derivative pedigrees have contaminated later claims from the earlier Hux association.

## Proof requirements
Reconstruct Hux households independently. Do not use Partin trees to prove Hux relationships and then use those Hux relationships to prove the Partin tree.

## Potential payoff
If independently verified, this would become one of the strongest non-surname migration signals in the project.

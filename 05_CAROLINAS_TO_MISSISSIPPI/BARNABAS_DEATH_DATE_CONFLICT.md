# Barnabas Partin — Death Date Conflict

Current derivative sources disagree on Barnabas/Barnaby's death:

- old 1997 Halifax research query: **24 Mar 1855**, Wilkinson County, Mississippi;
- WikiTree suggestion data surfaces both **24 Mar 1855** and **4 Mar 1855**;
- birth year likewise appears as 1772 or 1773.

## Consequence
Do not encode a day-level birth/death date as proved.

## Search window
For Wilkinson probate, search at least:
**1 Jan 1855 through 31 Dec 1856**, then expand if necessary.

## Goal
Use administration/probate date to establish a terminus and then locate an obituary, grave/church record, family Bible or estate language for the actual death date.

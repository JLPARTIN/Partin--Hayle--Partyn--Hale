# Barnabas/Barnaby Partin — 1855 Wilkinson County Probate Target

The repeated death tradition places Barnabas/Barnaby in Wilkinson County, Mississippi, in March 1855.

## Retrieval scope
Search Wilkinson County:
- wills;
- probate/minute books;
- administrations;
- inventories;
- estate sales;
- final settlements;
- deeds around death;
- cemetery/church records.

## What can close
A probate can identify spouse, children, sons-in-law, residence, land and associates, providing a downstream anchor from which to work backward through South Carolina to Halifax.

## Name variants
Barnabas, Barnaby, Barney; Partin, Parton, Partain, Parten.

The 1855 date remains derivative until a record is recovered.

# Barnabas Partin — Working Dossier

## Current reconstruction
Barnabas is reported as a son of Halifax John Partin and is traditionally assigned a birth year around 1773. His later migration is associated with the Carolinas and Mississippi.

## Immediate proof tasks
1. Prove Barnabas as John's son from the 1795 will/probate.
2. Establish earliest independent adult record for Barnabas.
3. Identify wife and children from primary records.
4. Build land/tax/census sequence in North Carolina.
5. Identify move into South Carolina and exact district.
6. Identify first Mississippi appearance.
7. Compare neighbors at each move.

## Migration method
For each jurisdiction record:
- last appearance in old county;
- first appearance in new county;
- land disposal/acquisition;
- tax gap;
- FAN associates moving with him;
- ages/household composition.

## Warning
A matching name in a later state is not migration proof by itself.

# Barnabas Partin — Web Claim Audit

## Repeated derivative tradition
Multiple modern genealogy pages repeat a Barnabas/Barnaby Partin born 29 Aug 1772 or 1773 in Halifax and dying in March 1855 in Wilkinson County, Mississippi.

The one-year birth discrepancy is itself a warning that these are derivative traditions rather than independent proof.

## Additional claim requiring investigation
Some public trees assign Barnabas a wife **Margaret/Peggy Hux**. If true, this would make the Hux association persist into the next generation and would be extremely important. At present it is **UNPROVEN** and must not be incorporated as fact.

## Search priority
Recover marriage, census, deed, probate and cemetery/church evidence for Barnabas and any Margaret/Peggy Hux independently.

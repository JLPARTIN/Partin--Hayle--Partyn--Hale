# 15 April 1648 — Seward Headright List and the Partin Household

## Record lead
A compiled patent transcription reports that John Seward received 1,200 acres in Isle of Wight County on a branch of the Roanoke called Blackwater for transportation of 24 persons, including:

- Robert Partin
- Margret, his wife
- Robert Partin Jr.
- Avis
- Rebecca
- Debora, his daughters

## Why this matters
The list appears to preserve the same family structure known from the 1624/25 muster while adding **Debora**, a daughter not yet born in 1625.

However, headright names do not necessarily mean these people physically immigrated in 1648. Rights could be assigned, reused, recorded late, or reflect return passages. Therefore this is evidence of a patent claim involving the family names, not proof of a new 1648 migration.

## Geographic importance
The patent is associated with **Blackwater** in Isle of Wight County. The later 1706 Robert Partin land is described on the north side of **Blackwater Swamp** in Surry. Whether these are directly connected tracts must be mapped rather than assumed, but the repeated Blackwater geography deserves focused study.

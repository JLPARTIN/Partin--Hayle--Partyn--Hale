## Assessment
The old description of a broad “Jamestown-to-Surry gap” is too crude. We now have a potentially continuous **Shirley Hundred father/son sequence** extending from the 1624/25 muster into the 1655 land chain.

The remaining problem is not whether the surname disappears after Jamestown. It is identifying the later generations and demonstrating the path from this Shirley/Charles City population into the Robert who appears in Surry/Southwark Parish in 1706.

## Critical next interval
**1655 → 1706** is now the high-value bridge. The alleged 1677 inventory and any 1660s–1690s land/probate/tax records should be attacked as a single record cluster.

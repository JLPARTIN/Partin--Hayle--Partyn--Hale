# 9 August 1657 — Robert Partin in Elizabeth City County

A compiled patent digest reports that Capt. Francis Hooke received 100 acres in Elizabeth City County on 9 August 1657, the land being **in the tenure and occupation of Robert Partin and Gresham Covell**.

## Identity problem
This could refer to Robert Sr., Robert Jr., or another Robert. By 1657 both father and son may have been living. The record must not be assigned without further evidence.

## Why it matters
The 1637 40-acre lease was also in Elizabeth City County. If the 1657 occupation can be tied to the same tract or neighborhood, it may provide a twenty-year land-tenure sequence and help distinguish which Robert held which property.

## Search program
Compare the 1637 lease bounds—great house, widow Thompson, Robert Brashere, church—with the 1657 Hooke patent and neighboring patents. Search Gresham Covell as a FAN associate.

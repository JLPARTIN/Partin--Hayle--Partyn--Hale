# Thomas Hayle Case — 1626–1627 Timeline

## 4 June 1626
Reported General Court indictment of Thomas Hayle, age 21, son of Symon Hayle of St Mary Somerset, London, porter. Convicted on rape indictments and sentenced to death.

## 3 July 1627
Margaret Partin of Shirley Hundred is ordered whipped and to receive forty stripes for concealing the offense of Thomas Hayle, described as “lately executed.”

## Forensic problem
The surviving transcription tradition therefore places the conviction/sentence about thirteen months before Margaret's punishment. “Lately executed” may indicate execution occurred later than sentence, or the dating/transcription sequence may require correction.

## Research rule
Do not describe both proceedings simply as “the 1627 case.” Keep indictment/sentence and Margaret's later concealment order as separate dated records until the authoritative court minutes establish the sequence.

# 1655 Shirley Hundred — Robert Partin Senior and Junior

## Major continuity finding
A 10 March 1655 Charles City County patent to Capt. Daniel Luellin/Llewellyn, abstracted in the Virginia land-patent literature, describes a 63-acre parcel in Shirley Hundred beginning at land of Llewellyn that had been **lately purchased of Robert Partin, Senior and Robert Partin, Junior, and sold to Mr. John Meares**.

This is substantially more important than a generic “Robert Partin appears in 1655” statement.

## Why it matters
The 1624/25 muster placed Robert Partin at West & Shirley Hundred with an infant son Robert, four months old. Thirty years later, the land description explicitly distinguishes **Robert Partin Sr.** and **Robert Partin Jr.** in the same Shirley Hundred landscape.

That creates a strong chronological and geographic continuity hypothesis:
- Robert Sr. — born approximately 1588/89 from muster age;
- Robert Jr. — infant in Jan. 1624/25;
- Robert Sr. + Robert Jr. — jointly named in a Shirley Hundred land chain by 1655.

This does not automatically prove that the 1655 junior is the 1624 infant, but the combined name, generational designation, location and chronology make the identification substantially stronger than a same-name match.

## Land/FAN network in the 1655 patent
The larger Daniel Llewellyn patent also references Walter Aston, Edward Gardner, Edward Madison, Sergeant John Harris, John Meares, Michael Turpin, Joseph Royall, Dorothy Baker and others. These names should be used to reconstruct the Shirley neighborhood and prior conveyance chain.

# 1624/25 Muster — Robert Partin Household

## Checked result
Virtual Jamestown's searchable muster database records Robert Partin as:
- household head;
- West & Sherley Hundred, Charles Cittie;
- age 36;
- ship: *Blessinge*;
- arrival: June 1609;
- muster date: 22 January 1624/5.

The same household result identifies:
- Robert Partin, child, 4 months;
- Avis Partin, child, 5;
- Rebecca Partin, child, 2;
- Thomas Hale, servant, 20, arrived on the *George* in October 1623;
- Ellin Cooke, servant, 25, arrived on the *London Marchannt* in June 1620.

The detailed household record also reports one house, two pounds powder, twenty pounds lead, four pieces of armor, one snaphance, fifty bushels corn, three neat cattle, one swine and nine poultry.

## Immediate correction to older derivative transcriptions
Some derivative publications scramble the children's ages or give Thomas Hale's arrival year incorrectly. The Virtual Jamestown database currently gives **Thomas Hale — George — October 1623**. This repository will use the database transcription provisionally while seeking the underlying muster image/edition.

## Genealogical implications
Robert's stated age yields an approximate birth year of 1588/89. Thomas's age yields approximately 1604/05. Those calculations independently match the current London candidate dates closely, but age agreement does not prove transatlantic identity.

Thomas is explicitly classified as a **servant** in the muster. That fact must be retained even if a kinship hypothesis is later proven; kin and servant status are not mutually exclusive.

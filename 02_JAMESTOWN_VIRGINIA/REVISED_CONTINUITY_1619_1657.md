## Revised assessment
The immigrant household does not vanish after 1625. A substantial land-record chain now appears to carry the family through the 1640s and 1650s.

The most important new target is the **13 April 1642 conveyance**, because the derivative digest reportedly says Robert the younger was Robert the elder's **son**.

## Remaining bridge
The hard problem shifts again: identify the fate of the younger Robert after 1655/57 and determine whether the Southwark Parish Robert of 1706 is that man, his son, or a collateral relative.

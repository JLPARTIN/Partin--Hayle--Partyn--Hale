# Robert Partin and Thomas Hayle — Virginia Anchor File

## Core evidence
The 1624/25 Virginia muster is the central transatlantic anchor in the present reconstruction. Project research records Robert Partin at about age 36, with arrival on the *Blessing* in June 1609, and Thomas Hale/Hayle at about age 20 in the household.

The ages independently point toward births around 1588/89 and 1604/05. This makes the London Robert Partyn (1589) and St Mary Somerset Thomas Hayle (1605) candidates chronologically precise enough to deserve full identity testing.

## Why Thomas matters
Virginia court evidence in the project reportedly identifies Thomas as the son of Symon Hayle of London. The exact court transcription, date, spelling, occupational language and procedural context must be attached before a final proof argument is written.

## Migration sequence under test
- Robert: 1609.
- Margaret: reconstructed 1617.
- Thomas: reconstructed 1623.
- Robert and Thomas together by the 1624/25 muster.

A staggered sequence followed by household convergence is compatible with chain migration, but chain migration must be demonstrated rather than presumed.

## Required source recovery
1. Full muster image/transcription.
2. Complete Thomas Hayle court proceedings.
3. Arrival/vessel evidence for Margaret and Thomas.
4. Land, patent, company, employment and household evidence for Robert.
5. Records explaining Thomas's relationship to the household.

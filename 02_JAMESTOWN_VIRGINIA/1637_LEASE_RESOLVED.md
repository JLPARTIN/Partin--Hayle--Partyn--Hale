# 24 November 1637 — Robert Partin's Elizabeth City Lease

## Record now substantially resolved
Nugent's *Cavaliers and Pioneers* abstract gives:
**Robert Partin, 40 acres, Elizabeth City, 24 Nov 1637, p. 503.** The tract lay east against Widow Thompson, south on the Fort Field, west on Robert Brasheare, and north toward the church. It was a **21-year lease**.

A separate published local history, *History of Hampton and Elizabeth City County, Virginia* (1922), independently uses this Robert Partin grant to locate the first church/glebe near Fort Field.

## Why this improves the chain
This is no longer merely an index hit. We now have the date, acreage, county, term and four boundary references, plus independent historical-geography confirmation.

## FAN / geography

The Brasseur family later appears farther south in the Nansemond corridor, making this neighbor worth following.

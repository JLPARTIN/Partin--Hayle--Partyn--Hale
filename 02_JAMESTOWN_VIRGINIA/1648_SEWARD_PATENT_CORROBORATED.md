# 15 April 1648 — Seward Patent Corroboration

The John Seward patent is now supported by two independent derivative transcriptions. It reports **1,200 acres** in Isle of Wight County on a branch called Blackwater, for transportation of 24 persons, among them:
Robert Partin; Margaret his wife; Robert Partin Jr.; Avis, Rebecca and Deborah his daughters.

## Important interpretation
The list is strikingly consistent with the known 1624/25 family, but a headright patent does not prove a 1648 immigration date. Headrights could be assigned or claimed later.

## Genealogical value
The cluster nevertheless preserves the family as a named unit and adds Deborah to the known daughters.

## Geographic value
It places the Partin names in a patent associated with Blackwater. This should be compared to later Surry Blackwater records, but identical water-name terminology is not proof of identical land.

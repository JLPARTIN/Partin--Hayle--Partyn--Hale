# Virginia Partin Surname-Control Audit, 1662–1683

## Purpose
Test a hidden assumption in the Jamestown→Surry reconstruction: that a later Virginia Partin must descend from Robert of Jamestown because the surname was unique.

The surviving patent/headright tradition does not support that assumption.

## Reported Partin-type headrights
A compiled genealogy reproduces the following patent-book entries:
- 11 Mar 1662 — **Samll. Partin**, Northumberland County patent to Dennis Eyes/Dennis Conway;
- 28 Nov 1662 — **Samll. Partin**, New Kent patent;
- 1 Oct 1663 — **Mary Partin**;
- 18 Nov 1663 — **Wm. Partin**, Rappahannock;
- 3 Apr 1663 — **Edw. Partin**;
- 30 Aug 1664 — **Mary Partin**, James City;
- 25 Sep 1665 — **Francis Partin**, Rappahannock;
- 9 Oct 1672 — **Jane Pantin/Partin**, Northampton;
- 27 Jan 1683 — **Mary Partan**, Gloucester.

The Samuel 1662 Northumberland entry is independently reproduced in another patent transcription as one of six transported persons.

## Interpretation caution
Headright appearance does not necessarily equal arrival on the patent date. Rights could be assigned, delayed or reused. Duplicate names may represent repeated claims for the same person.

## Genealogical consequence
The surname existed in several Virginia patent contexts outside Robert's documented household. Therefore:
**surname continuity is not pedigree continuity.**

Every later Robert/John/William/Charles must be connected through documentary identity evidence, geography, FAN networks and/or Y-DNA rather than assumed to descend from the 1609 immigrant.

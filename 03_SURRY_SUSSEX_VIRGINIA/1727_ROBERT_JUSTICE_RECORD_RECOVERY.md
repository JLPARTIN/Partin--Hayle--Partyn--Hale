# 1727 Robert Partin → John Justice — Record Recovery

## Newly recovered citation
A recent, source-footnoted Surry/Justice study gives the transaction as:

**Surry County, Virginia, Deeds, Wills, Etc. Book 7, Part 3 (1715–1730), pp. 726–727.**

Date: **13 March 1727**.

Parties:
- Robert Partin of Southwark Parish, Surry County
- John Justice of Martins Brandon Parish, Prince George County

Property:
- **200 acres on Blackwater Swamp**
- adjoining the lands of **Richard Washington**

This is a substantial upgrade from the earlier generic “March 1727” target because the exact volume/part/pages and an adjoining owner are now known.

## Next comparison
Compare the metes/bounds against the 1706 John Rawlings→Robert Partin 200-acre Blackwater deed. Same acreage and watershed are suggestive, but not proof of identical land.

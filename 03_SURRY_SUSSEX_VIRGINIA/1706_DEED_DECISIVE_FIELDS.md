# 1706 Rawlings → Robert Partin — Decisive Fields

When Deeds No.5 p.348 is opened, capture these fields verbatim:

1. grantor and grantee residence/parish;
2. consideration;
3. acreage;
4. full metes and bounds;
5. adjoining owners;
6. patent recital, including Jonathan/John Rawlings and 29 Apr 1692;
7. wife/relinquishment language;
8. witnesses;
9. acknowledgment/recording date;
10. any phrase indicating gift, love/affection or kinship.

## Why consideration matters
A nominal/familial consideration could materially strengthen the marriage hypothesis. Market-price consideration would still fit a son-in-law purchase but would carry less kinship signal.

No conclusion should be entered before the original wording is read.

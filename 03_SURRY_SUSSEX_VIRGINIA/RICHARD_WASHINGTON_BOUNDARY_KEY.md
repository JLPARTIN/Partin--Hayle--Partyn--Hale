# Richard Washington — Boundary Key

Richard Washington is now a high-value non-Partin research target.

He appears as an adjoining owner in:
- Gregory Rawlings's 1724 Blackwater transaction;
- Robert Partin's 1727 Blackwater transaction.

## Research strategy
Recover Washington deeds/patents spanning roughly 1690–1740. Boundary recitals may preserve earlier owners and solve which Rawlings parcel passed into Partin hands.

## Why neighbor deeds matter
Colonial deed books often preserve a tract's history more clearly in a neighbor's later conveyance than in the target family's surviving records. Search Washington, Cooper, Justice, Shockey and Rawlings together.

# Elizabeth Rawlings / “Eliza. Partin” — Identity Trial

## Secure anchor
John Rawlings's will, made Aug 1702 and proved 25 May 1703, is transcribed as naming his daughter **“Eliza. Partin.”**

## Candidate husband: Robert Partin
The strongest current circumstantial model is that Elizabeth was wife of Robert Partin:
- John Rawlings's daughter already bore the Partin surname by 1702/03.
- John Rawlings conveyed the 200-acre Blackwater tract to **Robert Partin** in 1706.
- A later 1727 Robert Partin deed reportedly has **Elizabeth** relinquishing dower.

If the 1727 dower record concerns the same Robert and tract, the three records form a coherent marriage/property chain.

## Why this is not yet PROVEN
We have not yet inspected the original 1702 will or the 1727 dower entry. “Eliza. Partin” could theoretically have married another Partin male. The exact land descriptions must be compared.

## Prediction
If Elizabeth Rawlings = wife of Robert:
1. the 1727 deed should name Robert's wife Elizabeth;
2. its tract should trace to or interact with Rawlings land;
3. Rawlings relatives may witness/neighbor Robert's deeds;
4. Charles Partin's later Rawlings-tenure land may reflect inheritance or family conveyance.

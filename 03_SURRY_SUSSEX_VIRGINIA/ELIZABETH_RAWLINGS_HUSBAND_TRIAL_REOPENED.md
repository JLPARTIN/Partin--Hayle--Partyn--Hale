# Elizabeth Rawlings — Husband Trial Reopened

## Proven/strong
John Rawlings's 1702/03 will names **daughter Eliza. Partin**.

## Model A — Robert Partin
Evidence:
- 1706 John Rawlings→Robert Partin, 200 acres Blackwater;
- Robert's 1727 Blackwater sale to John Justice;
- reported Elizabeth dower;
- Gregory Rawlings / Robert land neighborhood.

## Model B — Charles Partin
Newly strengthened evidence:
- Rawlings compilation says John III's inherited land was sold in two pieces in 1706, **one to his brother-in-law Charles Partin**;
- 1727 Charles reportedly sells that land, calling it “late in the tenure of John Rawlings.”

## Decisive records
1. 1706 Robert deed.
2. 1706 Charles deed.
3. 1727 Robert deed + Elizabeth acknowledgment.
4. 1727 Charles deed + any wife acknowledgment.

## Current judgment
**Elizabeth's husband is unresolved.**
Robert remains plausible, but Charles is now a serious competing candidate rather than a peripheral alternative.

# Rawlings Sibling Structure — 1702/03

John Rawlings's will provides a compact sibling group:

- **John Rawlings** — son; receives Blackwater land.
- **Gregory Rawlings** — son; receives home plantation, 126 acres.
- **Eliza. Partin** — daughter; already possesses some property.
- **Mary Rawlings** — widow/executrix.

## Research significance
This changes the Gregory question. Gregory is not merely a same-surname neighbor: the will explicitly makes Gregory and Eliza. Partin siblings.

Therefore the 1724 Gregory Rawlings Blackwater-area deed is potentially the land activity of **Elizabeth Partin's brother**.

## Remaining identity test
We must verify that the Gregory in the 1724 Gregory & Hannah Rawlings deed is the son named in the 1702 will. Chronology and land context favor the hypothesis, but identity should be demonstrated rather than assumed.

## New working hypothesis
If 1724 Gregory is the will's Gregory, then Robert Partin's 1727 tract adjoining the same Richard Washington neighborhood places Robert in the land network of Elizabeth's brother.

# Charles Partin — 1706 Rawlings Land Breakthrough

## New finding
The Rawlings research compilation contains a more specific statement than our earlier notes: it says that in **1706 John Rawlings III sold his inherited land in two pieces, one to his brother-in-law Charles Partin**.

The same compilation later says Charles sold that land in 1727, describing it as **“late in the tenure of John Rawlings.”**

## Why this matters
This materially changes the Elizabeth Rawlings husband trial.

Previously Charles was a competing possibility because of the 1727 Rawlings-land wording. The new statement makes the proposed relationship explicit in the research compilation: **Charles Partin is identified there as John Rawlings III's brother-in-law.**

If that description derives from the original 1706 deed or a reliable contemporary relationship statement, then Charles—not Robert—could be the husband of Elizabeth Rawlings.

# Rawlings 1706 — Two-Parcel Model

The Rawlings compilation states John Rawlings III disposed of inherited land in **two pieces** in 1706, including one piece to Charles Partin.

Separately, the documented research trail has John Rawlings→Robert Partin, 200 acres on Blackwater, recorded 7 May 1706.

## New question
Were Robert and Charles each grantees of different portions of Rawlings family land in the same year?

If yes, the Partin–Rawlings relationship may involve more than one Partin man and cannot be reconstructed from the Robert deed alone.

## Required reconstruction
Build a 1706 grantor table:
- John Rawlings→Robert Partin
- John Rawlings→Charles Partin
- acreage
- parcel origin
- consideration
- boundaries
- witnesses
- recording date

Then trace each parcel separately through 1727.

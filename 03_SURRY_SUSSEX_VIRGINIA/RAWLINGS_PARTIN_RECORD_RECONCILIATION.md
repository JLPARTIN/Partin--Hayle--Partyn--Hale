# Rawlings–Partin Record Reconciliation

## 1702/03 will
A Rawlings compilation transcribes John Rawlings's will as naming **“Daughter, Eliza. Partin”**, made Aug 1702 and proved 25 May 1703, Book 5 p.279.

## 1706 land
The same compilation gives:
Surry Deeds No.5 (1694–1709), p.348 — John Rawlings of Southwark Parish to Robert Partin of same, **200 acres north side Blackwater Swamp**, land taken up by Johnathan Rawlings by patent 29 Apr 1692; recorded 7 May 1706.

## 1727 complication
The Rawlings compilation separately states that **Charles Partin** sold land described as “late in the tenure of John Rawlings.” A different source-footnoted study proves a **Robert Partin→John Justice** 200-acre Blackwater conveyance on 13 Mar 1727, Book 7 Part 3 pp.726–727.

These must not be collapsed into one transaction without originals. There may be two Partin-held Rawlings-associated tracts or a derivative-summary error.

## Research consequence
The Elizabeth Rawlings = Robert's wife hypothesis remains strong, but the land argument now requires parcel-level reconstruction.

# John Partin — Virginia-to-Halifax Identity Problem

## 1746 Virginia anchor
A John Partin reportedly receives 592 pounds tobacco in the Aug 1746 Robert Partin estate context.

## Halifax sequence
A John Partin appears in the later Halifax/Edgecombe reconstruction from the 1760s onward.

## Competing identity models
A. 1746 Virginia John = later Halifax John.
B. Halifax John is a younger relative/son of the 1746 John.
C. Same surname, different branch.

## Chronology problem
The internet tradition often assigns Halifax John a birth around 1722, while another candidate “John Charles” baptism dated 16 Mar 1733 has been discussed. Neither should control the identification until primary records establish ages/relationships.

## Decisive evidence
- wording of the 1746 estate payment;
- Virginia land/tax records 1746–1762;
- Halifax 1762 tax;
- 1767 Hux deed;
- 1772 Fort deed;
- 1774 road order;
- 1795 will/probate;
- FAN surname continuity.

# Anna Jordan Will — Network Test

Derivative abstract, Surry Will Book 5 p.147:
- servant boy to John Rawlings;
- clothing to Elizabeth Rawlings;
- other bequests involving Shorsby/Sowerby-associated names;
- Mary Rawlings witness;
- execution/probate dates displayed confusingly.

## Hypothesis
The John, Elizabeth and Mary may belong to the household of John Rawlings d.1702/03 and Mary Sowerby.

## Why plausible
The compiler's family reconstruction places Mary Sowerby as John's wife, with children John, Elizabeth and Gregory.

## Why not proved
The abstract does not state the relationships among the Rawlings names.

- exact testator surname and marital history;
- exact Shorsby/Sowerby spellings;
- whether Mary signs or marks;

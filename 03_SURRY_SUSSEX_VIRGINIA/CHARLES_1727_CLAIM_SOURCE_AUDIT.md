# Charles Partin 1727 “Late in the Tenure” Claim — Source Audit

The Rawlings webpage repeatedly states that in 1727 Charles Partin sold land described as “late in the tenure of John Rawlings.”

However, unlike the Robert 1706 deed, the page does **not** provide an exact book/page citation in the displayed narrative.

## Critical problem
The same webpage alternates between Robert and Charles when describing the 1706 recipient and later says John may have overseen the land “for Robert or Charles Partin.”

Therefore the phrase “that land” cannot safely be used to assert parcel identity.

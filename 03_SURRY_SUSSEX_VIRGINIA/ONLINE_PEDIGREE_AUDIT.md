# Online Pedigree Audit — Unsupported Robert Numbering

## Common internet pedigree
Multiple user-contributed pages circulate a sequence roughly:
Robert I (immigrant) → Robert II (1624–1687) → Robert III (1655–1727) → Robert IV (1680–1743) → John (1722–1795).

Some also attach “Mary Alice Reviea” to Robert II and “Elizabeth Rawlings” to Robert III.

## Audit result
The current web search did **not** locate primary documentation supporting the precise birth/death dates or the numbered Robert III/IV parentage sequence. Ancestry's surfaced profile explicitly describes its material as user-contributed public trees. Other biography pages repeat the same dates without primary citations.

These claims therefore remain **UNPROVEN TREE DATA**.

## What is actually stronger
The documentary/patent trail gives us:
- an immigrant Robert;
- a son Robert in the 1624/25 household;
- a derivative 1642 abstract reportedly calling Robert younger the elder's son;
- Senior/Junior Partins in a 1655 Shirley land chain;
- a later Robert in Southwark Parish/Surry by 1706.

The responsible task is to bridge those anchors, not insert internet-generated “Robert III” and “Robert IV” generations because their dates look convenient.

## 1677/1687 warning
Current searches did not substantiate the previously inherited “1677 inventory” claim. Internet pages more commonly assert a death date of 14 June 1687 for Robert Jr., but likewise without adequate primary sourcing in the surfaced results. Neither date should be promoted until an actual probate/court record is found.

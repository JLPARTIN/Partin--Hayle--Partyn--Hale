# Charles Partin 1706 Deed — Existence Downgraded

Previous bundles treated a separate 1706 John Rawlings→Charles Partin deed as a likely record to recover.

A full source audit now shows that assumption came from an internally inconsistent derivative webpage.

The page's John III chronology names Charles, but its Elizabeth notes substitute Robert in the same sentence and then cite the known Robert deed, Surry Deeds No.5 p.348.

## New status
**Separate Charles 1706 deed: X/C- unverified.**

We should search the deed index for one, but must not state that such a deed existed until found.

## Known 1706 record
John Rawlings of Southwark Parish → Robert Partin of same, 200 acres north side Blackwater Swamp, land patented by father Johnathan Rawlings 29 Apr 1692; recorded 7 May 1706; Surry Deeds No.5 p.348.

## Research rule
Search Charles as a falsification test, not as retrieval of a presumed known deed.

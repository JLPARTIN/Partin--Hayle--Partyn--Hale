# John Rawlings — 1739 Boundary Lead

A derivative transcription reports:

**17 Mar 1739**, Edward Ellis of Southwark Parish → Marmaduke Chatham, 170 acres, a patent granted to John Rawlings 20 Oct 1689, bounded by Chatham, Thomas Allstin's / late Joshua Nicolson's land, and **John Rawlings**.

Citation given: Surry Deeds, Wills, Etc. #9 (1738–1754), p.119; recorded 19 Mar 1739.

## Value
This demonstrates that Rawlings boundary references persist after the 1706/1727 transactions and supplies another tract/patent date for parcel reconstruction.

## Identity caution
The 1739 “John Rawlings” boundary could be an older boundary name or a living younger John. Do not assign generation without the original.

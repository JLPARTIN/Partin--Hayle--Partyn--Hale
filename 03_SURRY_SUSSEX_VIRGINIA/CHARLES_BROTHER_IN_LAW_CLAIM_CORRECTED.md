# Charles Partin “Brother-in-Law” Claim — Corrected

## Exact source context
The Gregory Rawlings compilation writes that John III sold inherited land in two pieces in 1706, with one going to:

**“his brother in law (I THINK) Charles Partin”**

The capitalization and parenthetical show the author is proposing an identification, not quoting kinship language from the deed.

## Evidence grade
**C — researcher inference.**

## What remains useful
The same page reports a 1727 Charles Partin sale of land described as **“late in the tenure of John Rawlings.”** That land connection is worth recovering from the deed.

## What cannot be claimed
We cannot say:
- the 1706 deed calls Charles a brother-in-law;
- Charles is proved to have married Elizabeth Rawlings;
- the source independently establishes their marriage.

The original deed remains decisive.

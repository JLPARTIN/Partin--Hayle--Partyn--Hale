# Blackwater Parcel Cluster, 1724–1755

## New neighborhood reconstruction
A source-footnoted study of the Justice family supplies records surrounding the 1727 Robert Partin→John Justice tract.

### 1724
Gregory and Hannah Rawlings → William Cooper, 489 acres, adjoining **Richard Washington and Richard Shockey**.
Citation: Surry Deeds/Wills Etc. Book 7 Part 2, pp.588–589.

### 1727
Robert Partin → John Justice, 200 acres on Blackwater Swamp, adjoining **Richard Washington**.
Citation: Book 7 Part 3, pp.726–727.

### 1754
John and Henry Person → Richard Wiggins Jr., 213 acres adjoining **William Cooper, John Justice, John Nicholson and Benjamin Ricker**.
Citation: Surry Deed Book 7 (1753–1760), pp.128–130.

### 1755
Richard and Tabitha Wiggins → Michael Carsley, same 213-acre neighborhood, adjoining **William Cooper, John Justice, Benjamin Rickes and John Nicholson**.
Citation: pp.187–188.

## New inference
The 1727 Partin tract sits in a reconstructable Rawlings–Washington–Cooper–Justice neighborhood. The appearance of Gregory Rawlings nearby strengthens the case that parcel-level Rawlings/Partin analysis can resolve the 1706→1727 chain.

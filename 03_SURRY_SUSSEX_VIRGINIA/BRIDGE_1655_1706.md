# Bridge Problem — Shirley Hundred 1655 to Surry 1706

## Revised problem
The evidence now supports shifting the central bridge forward. A Robert Sr./Robert Jr. pair appears in the 1655 Shirley Hundred land chain. A Robert Partin is later documented in Southwark Parish, Surry County, in the 1706 Rawlings conveyance.

The key question is therefore:

**Who is the 1706 Robert relative to the 1655 Senior/Junior pair?**

## Candidate models
### Model A — 1706 Robert = 1655 Robert Jr.
The 1624 infant would be about 81 in 1706. Possible, but late-life land acquisition would need to fit later records and death chronology.

### Model B — 1706 Robert is a son of 1655 Robert Jr.
This creates an intervening generation consistent with normal chronology and many online pedigrees, but requires an actual parentage record.

### Model C — collateral Partin line
The Surry man could derive from another colonial Partin branch. This must remain open until the intervening records are reconstructed.

## Records capable of deciding
- 1655 conveyance underlying the Llewellyn patent;
- 1637 lease;
- alleged 1677 inventory;
- Charles City/Henrico/Surry probate;
- patents and deeds 1655–1706;
- tithables and parish material;
- land adjacency showing migration along the James/Blackwater corridor;
- recurring FAN associates.

## Warning
Birth years such as “Robert III born 1655” from user-contributed trees are not evidence and should not be used to manufacture Model B.

# Robert Partin Estate — 1746 New Lead

## Newly recovered derivative reference
A digitized genealogical compilation of Surry orphan/guardian accounts contains the entry:

**“Estate of Robert Partin owes Mr Claiborne 150 pounds of tobacco, 1746.”**

The compilation places this under **Surry Orphans Accounts 1744–1762**, with a page reference shown as **28** in the extracted list.

## Importance
This is independent evidence that a Robert Partin estate was active in 1746. It also identifies **Claiborne** as an estate creditor/associate who should be traced in the full account.

## Caution
The scan is a derivative compilation and its formatting is imperfect. Verify volume title, page number, clerk, and exact wording against the original.

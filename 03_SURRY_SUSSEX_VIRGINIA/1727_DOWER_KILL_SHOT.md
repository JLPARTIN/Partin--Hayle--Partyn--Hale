# 1727 Elizabeth Dower — Identity Kill Shot

The reported June 1727 Elizabeth dower acknowledgment is now the highest-value single Surry identity record.

## If it says
**Elizabeth, wife of Robert Partin**, relinquished dower in the same 200-acre tract Robert conveyed to John Justice on 13 Mar 1727:

then Robert's wife Elizabeth is directly proved.

## Combined proof chain
John Rawlings will → daughter Eliza. Partin
+
1706 John Rawlings → Robert Partin Blackwater land
+
1727 Robert Partin → John Justice Blackwater land
+
Elizabeth identified as Robert's wife in acknowledgment

This would create a very strong Elizabeth Rawlings = wife of Robert Partin case, especially if parcel identity is also proved.

## Caveat
Even a wife named Elizabeth does not by itself prove she was Rawlings's daughter; the land/family chain supplies the identification argument.

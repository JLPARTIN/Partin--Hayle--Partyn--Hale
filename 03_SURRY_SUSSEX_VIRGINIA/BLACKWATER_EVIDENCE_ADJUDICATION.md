# Blackwater Evidence Adjudication

## Known derivative chain

### 1706
John Rawlings → Robert Partin
- 200 acres;
- north side Blackwater Swamp;
- land reportedly from father Johnathan/John Rawlings's 29 Apr 1692 patent;
- Deeds No.5 p.348.

### 1724
Gregory & Hannah Rawlings → William Cooper
- 489 acres;
- adjoining Richard Washington and Richard Shockey;
- Book 7 Part 2 pp.588–589.

### 1727
Robert Partin → John Justice
- 200 acres;
- Blackwater Swamp;
- adjoining Richard Washington;
- Book 7 Part 3 pp.726–727.

### 1754–1755
Later deeds place William Cooper and John Justice as adjoining owners.

## Inference
Robert's grantee and Gregory Rawlings's grantee occupied adjoining parts of the same reconstructable neighborhood. Richard Washington is a shared boundary anchor.

## What is not yet proved
The 1706 200 acres and 1727 200 acres have not yet been demonstrated from originals to be the identical tract.

## Decisive test
Compare:
- source-of-title recital;
- acreage;
- watercourse;
- boundary owners;
- courses/distances;
- patent references.

If 1706→1727 same-parcel identity is proved and Elizabeth's 1727 dower is verified, the case that Robert was the Partin husband operating inside Elizabeth Rawlings's immediate sibling land network becomes substantially stronger.

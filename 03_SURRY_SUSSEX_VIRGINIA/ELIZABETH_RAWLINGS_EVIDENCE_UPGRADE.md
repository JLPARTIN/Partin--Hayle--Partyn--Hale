# Elizabeth Rawlings → Partin — Evidence Upgrade

## What is now strong
Two independent derivative/publication paths preserve John Rawlings's 1702/03 will naming **daughter Eliza. Partin**.

The will also identifies her brothers John and Gregory.

## What remains unresolved

# Elizabeth Rawlings → Partin Marriage Window

## Secure upper bound
John Rawlings's will made Aug 1702 calls her **Eliza. Partin**. She was married by then.

## Potential lower anchor
A 1697 Surry will abstract reportedly names **Elizabeth Rawlings** and leaves her clothing.

If original records establish that this is the same woman, the surname transition would narrow her marriage to roughly **1697–Aug 1702**.

## Search strategy
Search Surry marriage/church/court/deed records in that interval for:
- Elizabeth/Eliza Rawlings/Rawlins/Rollings;
- Partin/Parton/Parten;
- Robert;
- Charles.

Also search deeds where a married woman relinquishes dower or where John/Mary Rawlings convey property to a Partin household.

This time window may be more productive than searching generic online pedigrees.

# Surry 1706 — Robert Partin and the Rawlings Land

## Record lead
A Rawlings-family compilation quotes **Surry County Deeds No. 5 (1694–1709), p. 348**:

John Rawlings of Southwark Parish, Surry, conveyed to Robert Partin of the same parish **200 acres on the north side of Blackwater Swamp**, land said to have been taken up by Rawlings's father Jonathan/John Rawlings by patent dated **29 April 1692**.

## Why this is important
This is not merely a surname occurrence. If the quoted citation is accurate it fixes:
- Robert's parish: Southwark;
- a tract size: 200 acres;
- a geographic feature: Blackwater Swamp;
- a grantor: John Rawlings;
- a prior-generation Rawlings patent;
- a precise deed-book target: Deeds No. 5, p. 348.

This gives the Surry investigation a concrete land/FAN anchor that can be compared with later deeds, neighbors, processioning, tithables and estate records.

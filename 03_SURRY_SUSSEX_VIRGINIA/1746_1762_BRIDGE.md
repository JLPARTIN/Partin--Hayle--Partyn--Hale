# Virginia → Halifax Bridge, 1746–1762

## Narrowed interval
The working chronology has a John Partin in Robert's 1746 Virginia estate context and a John Partin in the Halifax/Edgecombe tax environment by 1762.

That leaves a sixteen-year bridge.

## Identity hypothesis
**H1:** Virginia John and Halifax John are the same man.
Alternative:
**H2:** Halifax John is a younger close relative from the same Partin cluster.
**H3:** unrelated/collateral Partin.

## Tests
- Search Virginia deed/order/tithable records after 1746 for John's disappearance.
- Search NC records before 1762 for first appearance.
- Compare FAN surnames.
- Compare land geography and migration routes.
- Recover age/household clues from taxes, militia, jury/road records.
- Search for deeds stating former residence.

## Strongest form of proof
A deed, court record or land grant explicitly calling John “of Surry/Sussex County, Virginia” in a North Carolina transaction—or a Virginia conveyance naming a North Carolina residence.

# 1746 Robert Partin Estate — Generation Gate

## Existing compiled facts to verify
- Robert Partin dies / estate administration in Aug 1746.
- Mary acts as administratrix.
- John receives 592 pounds of tobacco in estate accounting.

## Questions the full probate can answer
1. Is Mary called widow/relict/wife?
2. Is John called son, heir, creditor, purchaser, laborer, or simply payee?
3. Who were securities on Mary's administration bond?
4. Who appraised the estate?
5. Who purchased estate goods?
6. Do Charles, William, Henry, Rawlings, Tucker, Justice or Hux appear?
7. Is land described?
8. Are minor children/guardianships present?

## Why this is the generation gate
The 1746 estate sits immediately before the Virginia-to-Halifax transition. A relationship word attached to John or Henry could materially alter the pedigree.

## Rule
The 592-pound tobacco payment does **not** make John a son unless the record says so or other evidence demonstrates it.

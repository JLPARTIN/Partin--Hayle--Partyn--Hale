# John Rawlings Will — Independent Published Abstract Upgrade

## Finding
A separate digitized Surry wills/administrations compilation independently abstracts the will of John Rawlings and confirms the crucial family structure:

- son John Rawlings receives all land at Blackwater;
- son Gregory Rawlings receives the home plantation, 126 acres, tools and books;
- **daughter Eliza. Partin** receives livestock and other property already in her possession;
- wife Mary is executrix;
- made Aug 1702;
- proved 25 May 1703;
- witnesses Robert Nicholson, Hannah Nicholson, Sampson Lainer;
- **Book 5, p.279**.

## Why this matters
The “Eliza. Partin” wording no longer rests only on the online Gregory Rawlings compilation. It is independently present in a published/digitized Surry will abstract.

## Evidentiary effect
This substantially strengthens the proposition that John Rawlings had a daughter married to a Partin by Aug 1702. It still does not identify her husband by given name.

Surry Will Book 5, p.279 remains the A-grade target.

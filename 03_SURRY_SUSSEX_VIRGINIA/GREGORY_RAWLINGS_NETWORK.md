# Gregory Rawlings — Blackwater Network

Gregory Rawlings and wife Hannah conveyed 489 acres in 1724 to William Cooper, adjoining Richard Washington and Richard Shockey.

Three years later Robert Partin's 200-acre Blackwater tract conveyed to John Justice also adjoined Richard Washington.

## Why this matters
John Rawlings's daughter was already “Eliza. Partin” by 1702/03. The presence of another Rawlings household in the same Blackwater neighborhood provides a new FAN and land-reconstruction route.

## Questions
- Relationship of Gregory to John Rawlings.
- Whether the 489-acre and 200-acre tracts share a boundary.
- Whether Richard Washington's land was the common boundary.
- Whether Cooper/Justice deeds recite earlier Rawlings patents.
- Whether Elizabeth Partin appears in acknowledgments.

## Status
**New geographic/FAN connection; kinship between Gregory and John Rawlings not yet established.**

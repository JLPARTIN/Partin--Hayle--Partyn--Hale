# Charles Partin — Surry/Sussex Dossier

## Known/reported anchors
- appears with Robert in Tucker estate material, 1721–22;
- associated with land in 1727 described in derivative research as “late in the tenure of John Rawlings”;
- remains a candidate close relative of Robert, but relationship is unproved.

## Why Charles matters
Charles may explain why Rawlings property appears in more than one Partin context. He also complicates the identity of John Rawlings's daughter “Eliza. Partin”: she cannot be assigned to Robert without excluding Charles and other adult Partin males.

## Research targets
- all Charles Partin deeds, court orders and tithables;
- Tucker estate entry;
- 1727 conveyance exact wording;
- wife/dower;
- probate;
- witnesses shared with Robert;
- land adjacency to Rawlings tract.

## Prohibited shortcut
Do not make Charles Robert's son/brother merely because online pedigrees do so.

# Elizabeth Rawlings — Identity Collision

There are multiple Elizabeth Rawlings women in Surry-era records.

## Elizabeth A — John Rawlings daughter → Partin
- father: John Rawlings, testator 1702/03;
- siblings named in will: John and Gregory;
- called **Eliza. Partin** by Aug 1702;
- husband first name unresolved.

## Elizabeth B — Roger & Alice (Newsom) Rawlings daughter
A published Virginia genealogical article reconstructs Roger and Alice Rawlings's daughter Elizabeth as:
1. wife of William Pettaway/Petway;
2. later wife of John Davis.

This line has its own documentary trail through Petway estate and a 1705 deed.

## Rule
Do not use records of Elizabeth Petway/Davis to fill gaps in Elizabeth Partin's biography.

Same surname + same county + same generation is not identity.

# John Rawlings Will — Property Analysis

The independent will abstract adds more than kinship.

## Distribution
- son John: all land at Blackwater;
- son Gregory: home plantation, 126 acres;
- daughter Eliza. Partin: livestock/property already in her possession;
- wife Mary: executrix.

## Important implication
Elizabeth was already married to a Partin by Aug 1702 and already possessed property associated with her father's distribution. The will does not say her husband received land.

Therefore the 1706 John Rawlings→Robert Partin deed should be treated as a **separate post-will transaction**, not automatically as Elizabeth's inheritance.

## Test
The deed's consideration and language may reveal whether it was:
- an ordinary purchase;
- a family conveyance;
- fulfillment of an earlier arrangement;
- transfer involving Elizabeth's marital household.

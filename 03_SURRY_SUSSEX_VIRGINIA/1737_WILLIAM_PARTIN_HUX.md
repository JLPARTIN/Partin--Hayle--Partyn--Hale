# 1737 — William Partin and Elizabeth Hux

A published Surry will abstract for an estate proved in 1737 lists witnesses including **Wm. Partin** and **Elizabeth Hux**.

## Why this matters
Decades later, the Halifax research trail reports William and Ann Hux conveying land to John Partin in 1767. The 1737 Surry co-occurrence of the Partin and Hux surnames raises a testable possibility that the later Halifax association reflects an older FAN connection rather than a new acquaintance.

## Caution
This is surname-network evidence only. Elizabeth Hux is not automatically connected to William/Ann Hux of the later Halifax deed, and William Partin's identity must be established.

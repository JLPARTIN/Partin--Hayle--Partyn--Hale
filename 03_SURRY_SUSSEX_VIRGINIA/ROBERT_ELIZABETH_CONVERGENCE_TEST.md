# Robert + Elizabeth — Convergence Test

The Robert-husband hypothesis should be judged on independent converging evidence, not surname coincidence.

## Existing convergence
1. John Rawlings names daughter Eliza Partin by 1702.
2. John Rawlings conveys 200 Blackwater acres to Robert Partin in 1706.
3. Robert sells 200 Blackwater acres to John Justice in 1727.
4. Reported Elizabeth acknowledgment/dower is associated with the 1727 transaction.
5. John Justice later occupies a neighborhood adjoining William Cooper.
6. William Cooper's relevant land came from Gregory Rawlings, Elizabeth's documented brother.
7. Richard Washington appears in both Gregory's 1724 and Robert's 1727 boundary networks.

## Missing decisive links
- original 1727 acknowledgment naming Elizabeth as Robert's wife;
- same-parcel proof between 1706 and 1727;
- exclusion/adjudication of Charles's 1727 Rawlings-connected transaction.

If the first two are confirmed, the Robert model rises sharply.

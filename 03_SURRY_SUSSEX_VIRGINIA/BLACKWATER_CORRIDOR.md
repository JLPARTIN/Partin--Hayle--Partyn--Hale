# Blackwater Corridor — Virginia Migration Hypothesis

## Evidence points
A 1648 patent transcription places a John Seward grant on a branch called Blackwater and includes the Partin household names among headrights.

The 1706 Surry deed lead places Robert Partin on a 200-acre tract on the north side of Blackwater Swamp, acquired from John Rawlings.

## Hypothesis
Rather than imagining a sudden unexplained relocation from Jamestown to Surry, the family may have moved through a broader James/Blackwater land corridor over multiple decades.

## Caution
“Blackwater” is a recurring geographic name and the two references cannot be equated without mapping the waterways and patent bounds.

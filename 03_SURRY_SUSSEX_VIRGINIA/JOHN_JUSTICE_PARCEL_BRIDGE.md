# John Justice Parcel Bridge

## 1727 anchor
Robert Partin of Southwark Parish → John Justice of Martins Brandon Parish, Prince George County, 13 Mar 1727:
- 200 acres;
- Blackwater Swamp;
- adjoining Richard Washington;
- Surry Deeds, Wills, Etc. Book 7 Part 3, pp.726–727.

## Later neighborhood evidence
A modern Surry land study cites:
- 1724 Gregory & Hannah Rawlings → William Cooper, 489 acres adjoining Richard Washington and Richard Shockey;
- 1754 Person → Richard Wiggins Jr., 213 acres adjoining **William Cooper and John Justice** plus Nicholson/Ricker;
- 1755 Wiggins → Michael Carsley, same 213-acre neighborhood adjoining **William Cooper and John Justice**.

## Significance
The man who bought Robert Partin's 1727 tract later appears as an adjoining owner to William Cooper, whose land came directly from Gregory Rawlings.

This is independent geographic evidence tying Robert's grantee into Gregory Rawlings's land neighborhood.

## Grade
**B+ land-network inference; A/B+ citations pending original images.**

It strengthens, but does not by itself prove, that Robert's tract was part of the Rawlings family land complex.

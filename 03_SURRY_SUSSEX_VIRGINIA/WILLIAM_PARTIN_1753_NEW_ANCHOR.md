# William Partin — New 1753 Surry Anchor

## Newly recovered record citation
A deed abstract identifies **William Partin as a witness on 30 Jan 1753**:

William Rochell → James Hearn, 100 acres in Albemarle Parish on south side of Raccoon Swamp, bounded by John Wilburn, Arthur Freeman and Hearn.

Witnesses:
- James Carter
- John (X) Stokes
- **William Partin**

Recorded 17 Jul 1753.

Citation: **Surry County Deed Book 7 (1753–1760), p.47.**

## Significance
William is therefore not merely a 1737 occurrence. He is independently present in Surry sixteen years later, strengthening the case for treating him as a durable adult member of the local Partin cluster.

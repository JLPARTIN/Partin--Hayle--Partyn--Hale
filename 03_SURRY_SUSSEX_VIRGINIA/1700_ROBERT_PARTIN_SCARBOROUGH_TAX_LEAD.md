# 1700 Robert Partin–Edward Scarborough Tax Lead

## Reported entry
A genealogical profile compiling Edward Scarborough's Surry County tax appearances reports:

**1700 — Edw Scarborough & Robt Partin — 2 polls.**

The surrounding sequence is unusually useful:
- 1695 Scarborough — 1 poll
- 1696 Scarborough — 1 poll
- 1698 Scarborough — 1 poll
- 1699 Scarborough — 1 poll
- **1700 Scarborough + Robert Partin — 2 polls**
- 1701 Scarborough — 1 poll
- 1702 Scarborough — 1 poll
- 1703 Scarborough — 1 poll

## Interpretation
Robert appears as the additional taxable in a single year of Scarborough's otherwise one-poll sequence.

Possible explanations include:
- resident laborer/servant;
- tenant;
- household associate;
- temporary co-resident;
- tax-list grouping convention;
- family/FAN relationship.

None can be selected without the original list and knowledge of the list's organization.

## Chronological importance
If genuine, this places a Robert Partin in Surry before:
- John Rawlings's Aug 1702 will naming Eliza Partin;
- the 1706 Rawlings→Robert land transaction.

It therefore demonstrates that Robert did not first appear in Surry merely because of the 1706 purchase.

## Grade
**C+/B- derivative lead pending original tax-list verification.**

## Required recovery
Find the original/transcribed Surry tithable list for 1700 and photograph:
- heading/date;
- precinct/list-taker;
- entries before and after Scarborough;
- exact spelling;
- column structure;
- poll total.

Also recover 1699 and 1701 pages to understand why Robert appears only in 1700.

# Robert Partin — Surry/Sussex Working Biography, 1706–1746

## 1706
Robert Partin of Southwark Parish receives 200 acres north side Blackwater Swamp from John Rawlings.

## 1721–22
Robert and Charles Partin occur in Tucker estate material in the existing research compilation. Original entries remain recovery targets.

## March–June 1727
Robert reportedly sells 200 acres to John Justice. A June 1727 record reportedly has Elizabeth relinquishing dower. These records are critical because they may identify Robert's wife as the Elizabeth Partin named in John Rawlings's will.

## 1746
Existing compilation places Robert with wife/associate Mary and records his death in Aug 1746 with Mary as administratrix. John receives 592 pounds tobacco in the estate context.

## Wife problem
If both 1727 Elizabeth and 1746 Mary are correctly attached to the same Robert, the simplest model is two wives in sequence. But separate same-name Roberts remain possible until the deeds/probate are read together.

## Identity controls
Do not assign:
- a precise birth year;
- “Robert III/IV” numbering;
- parents;
- Elizabeth Rawlings as wife;
until the corresponding evidence is recovered.

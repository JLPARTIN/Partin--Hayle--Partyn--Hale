# Gregory Rawlings ↔ Elizabeth Partin Land Network

## Family relationship
The 1702/03 John Rawlings will identifies Gregory Rawlings and Eliza. Partin as siblings.

## Land relationship to test
A 1724 Gregory & Hannah Rawlings conveyance adjoins Richard Washington.
Robert Partin's 1727 Blackwater conveyance also adjoins Richard Washington.

## Why this is stronger than a generic FAN link
If the 1724 Gregory is the same Gregory named in the will, the neighboring Rawlings land belongs to the **brother of Elizabeth Partin**, not merely an unrelated Rawlings household.

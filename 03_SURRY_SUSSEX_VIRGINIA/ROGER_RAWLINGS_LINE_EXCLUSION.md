# Roger Rawlings Line — Exclusion File

A Virginia genealogical article reports Roger and Alice (Newsom) Rawlings had a daughter Elizabeth who married William Pettaway/Petway and then John Davis.

This provides an important **negative identity control**.

## Why it matters
Search results for “Elizabeth Rawlings Surry” can easily surface this woman and be mistakenly attached to the Partin wife.

## Distinguishing markers
Elizabeth Petway/Davis:
- Roger + Alice Newsom parents;
- Petway children/estate;
- John Davis second husband;
- 1705 deed connected to Newsom/Rawlings inheritance.

Elizabeth Partin:
- father John Rawlings d.1702/03;
- brothers John and Gregory;
- already called Eliza Partin in father's will.

Keep the two women in separate evidence buckets unless a primary record forces revision.

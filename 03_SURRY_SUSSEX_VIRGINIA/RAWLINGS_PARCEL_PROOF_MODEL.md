# Rawlings–Partin Parcel Proof Model

The next Surry breakthrough should come from **land identity**, not name similarity.

## Fixed anchors
- 1706: John Rawlings → Robert Partin, 200 acres, north side Blackwater Swamp.
- 1724: Gregory & Hannah Rawlings → William Cooper, 489 acres adjoining Richard Washington.
- 1727: Robert Partin → John Justice, 200 acres on Blackwater Swamp adjoining Richard Washington.

## Test
If the 1706 deed's metes/bounds also place the tract against Richard Washington—or contain another boundary repeated in 1727—we can argue the 1727 sale disposed of the 1706 Rawlings tract.

## Consequence
If the related acknowledgment names Elizabeth as Robert's wife, the combined will + land + dower evidence would strongly identify John Rawlings's daughter “Eliza. Partin” as Robert's wife.

## Do not shortcut
Same acreage + same swamp is not enough. Compare boundary calls, neighbors, patent provenance, consideration and chain of title.

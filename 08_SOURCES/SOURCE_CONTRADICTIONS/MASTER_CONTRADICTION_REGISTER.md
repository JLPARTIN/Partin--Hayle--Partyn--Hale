# Master Contradiction Register

Every genuine factual contradiction surfaced during the September 2026 Google Drive research recovery is recorded here, with both/all versions preserved and their sources identified. Per this project's evidence standard, contradictions are never silently harmonized. Entries are grouped by generation/subject and cross-reference the claim they affect in `00_PROJECT_CONTROL/MASTER_CLAIM_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md`.

---

## 1. Margaret Partin's age at the 1624/25 muster

- **Version A:** age 36 (multiple independently recovered documents).
- **Version B:** age 56 (the "Brick•Wall•Broke" chat-log document, which uses both figures for Margaret in different places, and also gives Robert Sr.'s age as both 30 and 36 within the same document).

**Status:** Unresolved. Version A is corroborated by more independent sources. Requires the original muster image.

## 2. Margaret Partin's name at the 1624/25 muster

- **Version A:** "Margaret" (the overwhelming majority of recovered sources, and the version used consistently elsewhere in this project).
- **Version B:** "Rebecca" (one document's summary table only — almost certainly an error confusing the wife with a daughter named Rebecca who appears in the same document's own household list).

**Status:** Version B treated as a compiler error, not a competing fact, but preserved here for transparency.

## 3. Margaret Partin's maiden name

- **Version A:** Hayle (the project's long-standing prior working hypothesis).
- **Version B:** Not Hayle — the recovered 1625/26 will of Symon Hayle (London tailor, proposed father of the Virginia Thomas Hayle) names his children and does not include a daughter Margaret.

**Status:** Version B is new negative evidence recovered September 2026. See `MASTER_CLAIM_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md` Claim 3 — the Hayle maiden-name theory is now REJECTED pending new evidence.

## 4. The 1742 Virginia "inheritance" deed's evidentiary weight

- **Version A:** "GATE-PASSED... an image-verified hit of the highest priority" and "an extraordinary rarity in colonial genealogy," treated as near-definitive proof of a 1609-to-1740s lineage link (one narrative synthesis document).
- **Version B:** "unstable in current form... unresolved claim pending original retrieval... Do not promote until original inheritance wording is checked" (a separately recovered, explicitly conservative triage document covering the same record class).

**Status:** Unresolved; the two recovered documents flatly disagree about the same deed's evidentiary status. Version B's conservative standard governs per this project's evidence rules until the original is examined.

## 5. The 1746 Surry Co. VA estate payment to "John Partin"

- **Version A:** "592" (unit unstated, presumably pounds of tobacco).
- **Version B:** "3 Pounds, 15 Shillings."

**Status:** Unresolved. Both figures are reported by independent documents describing what both call the same transaction. Requires direct image review (one source cites FamilySearch ARK 3Q9M-C9PX-K6KN).

## 6. Whether the 1746 payment proves John Partin was Robert Partin's son

- **Version A:** Called a "Smoking Gun" and "primary evidence of heirship" (the project owner's own Jamestowne Society application draft).
- **Version B:** "does not state 'son John'" — explicitly inferential only (at least four independent documents, including both a Gemini and a ChatGPT parallel run of a dedicated parentage investigation, and a separate long-form audit transcript that calls this bridge "the biggest problem" in the whole research program).

**Status:** Unresolved; see `MASTER_CLAIM_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md` Claim 8. Version B governs under this project's standard.

## 7. Robert Partin (Surry Co., d. 1743/44)'s wife's name

- **Version A:** Elizabeth (named in a 1727 dower-relinquishment deed).
- **Version B:** Mary (granted Letters of Administration for the same Robert Partin's estate in late 1746).

**Status:** Unresolved — the compiling document itself flags this and offers two explanations (two successive wives, or a naming variance) without adopting either.

## 8. Who actually administered Robert Partin's (d.1743/44) estate

- **Version A:** Mary Partin, widow, granted Letters of Administration (late 1746 court order).
- **Version B:** Robert Connell, court-appointed, shown as the administrator on the actual estate account dated 20 Aug 1746.

**Status:** Unresolved — flagged, not reconciled, by the same document that reports both facts.

## 9. The 1795 Halifax Co. John Partin will — date

- **Version A:** 13 July 1795 (proved August 1795) — corroborated by the largest number of independent sources, including the Jim Partin book and the Opal App transcription log.
- **Version B:** 2 January 1795 — one narrative-synthesis document.

**Status:** Unresolved. Version A currently treated as the working date.

## 10. The 1795 Halifax Co. John Partin will — page citation

- **Version A:** Will Book 3, page 248 (multiple independent sources).
- **Version B:** Will Book 3, page 251 (a deterministic-scoring "FGIS" report).
- **Version C:** Will Book 3, page 256 (a testator-index citation appearing in one source, "NC Wills: A Testator Index 1665–1900," Will #530).

**Status:** Unresolved. This is the single highest-priority page-citation conflict in the project — direct image review of Halifax Co. Will Book 3 is needed to establish which (if any) of these three page numbers is correct.

## 11. The 1795 Halifax Co. John Partin will — heir list

- **Version A:** Sons Robert (Robin, executor), Brasel/Brasil, Barnabas, David, Charles; daughters Lucy, Sarah, Mary, Priscilla, Elizabeth. Corroborated across the largest number of independent AI-assisted research sessions, the Opal App transcription log, and the externally authored Jim Partin book.
- **Version B:** Sons "Bennet and Lewis" only, no daughters named — one narrative migration-research document, uncited.
- **Version C:** A completely different heir set: daughters Sarah Applewhite (m. Henry Applewhite), Elizabeth Sullivant, Martha Brantley, Jane Bird; sons Robert Brantley and Thomas Brantley; daughter Lucy Russel (m. Richard Russel); daughter Elizabeth Jackson ("daughter of my last wife"); sons "John & Richard" ("sons of my last wife") — one narrative document, which itself notes the Brantley/Jackson surnames suggest John Partin married a widow and raised stepchildren.

**Status:** Unresolved. This is the single highest-priority content conflict in the project. Version A is the working reading given its broader corroboration, but Version C in particular cannot be dismissed without checking the primary image, since it could reflect an entirely different Halifax testator whose record has been conflated into this file by a derivative index.

## 12. John Partin's (d. 1795) wife's name at different life stages

- 1761 Charles City Co. VA slander suit: wife named **Elizabeth**.
- 1795 Halifax will and death: wife named **Mary**.

**Status:** Unresolved. Three explanations offered across the recovered corpus, none adopted: an earlier first wife who predeceased him; a different, unrelated John Partin in the 1761 suit; or a scribal/transcription error in one of the two records.

## 13. John Partin's (d. 1795) wife's maiden name

**Status:** Version B governs. See `MASTER_CLAIM_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md` Claim 10 — REJECTED.

## 22. Robert Partin Sr.'s (and the London Partyn family's) place of origin — RESOLVED (owner-confirmed)

- **Version A:** London, specifically citing London Metropolitan Archives references placing a John Partin/Alice Gaskyn marriage at St. Botolph Aldgate (1586) and a Robert Partyn baptism at St. Margaret Pattens (1589); part of the same London research network as the St. Mary Somerset Hayle family material.
- **Version B:** Bristol/Gloucestershire/Somerset, with a marriage circa 1585–87 and a baptism dated 14 April 1588 — offered with no archival citation at all, only "likely."

**Status: RESOLVED.** The project owner has directly confirmed (Oct 2026) that Version B (Bristol/Gloucestershire/Somerset) was one of several unsourced working theories produced while manually working through conflicting, uncited online family trees, and is **not** backed by any record in their own notes — it should be disregarded, not treated as a live competing theory. This most likely originated from conflating the London parish name "St. Mary Somerset" (the Hayle family's parish) with the English county of Somerset or nearby Bristol — exactly the error this project's own `RESEARCH_STATUS.md` had already warned against in the abstract. **Version A (London, St. Mary Somerset-adjacent research network) is retained as the project's legitimate source-backed English-origins thread**, though its own specific citations (St. Botolph Aldgate, St. Margaret Pattens) remain CANDIDATE pending independent image verification — retained and pursued, not proven. See `01_ENGLAND_THAMES/LONDON_NETWORK.md` and `00_PROJECT_CONTROL/REJECTED_AND_QUARANTINED_CLAIMS.md` #7.

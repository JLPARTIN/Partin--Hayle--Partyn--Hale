# Appalachian Lines — Tennessee, Georgia, Kentucky, and Upland South Carolina (Collateral, 18th–19th c.)

## Executive Summary

**This dossier is explicitly collateral and comparative, not the project's direct line.** It gathers a set of Partin/Parton/Partain families documented in the upland South — East Tennessee, northeast Georgia, Kentucky, and the Anderson County, SC area — who are genetically related to the project's documented Halifax line at the broad R-M269 haplogroup level, and in one sub-cluster (R-FTE30394) form the single largest Y-DNA group recorded in the project's own raw testing data, but who have **no established documentary connection** to Robert Partin Sr., John Partin of Halifax, or Barnabas Partin. This dossier exists so that scattered mentions of these families across the project's research corpus are gathered in one place rather than left to clutter the Virginia and Halifax dossiers, and so that future DNA-to-documentary correlation work has a single home. Unlike the England-through-Mississippi chapters, this dossier is a **preliminary characterization**, not a mature, fully cross-checked narrative — the underlying material is thinner and was never previously organized as a unit.

## Why This Grouping Exists

The project's own raw Y-DNA spreadsheet (see `09_DNA/YDNA_MASTER_DOSSIER.md`) shows its **dominant terminal SNP, R-FTE30394, in 8 of 19 rows**, associated with self-reported family origins in Sevier County, Cocke County, and Franklin County, Tennessee, and Haywood County, North Carolina — all Appalachian/upland counties, geographically and culturally distinct from the Tidewater-to-Piedmont-to-Halifax corridor that defines the rest of this project. Two further clusters in the same dataset — R-BY31280 (Wilson Co., TN) and R-FTB85652 (Maury Co., TN) — are also Middle/East Tennessee. A fifth, R-Y32699 (Anderson Co., SC, 2 kits), sits in the upland South Carolina piedmont near the Georgia line. **None of these clusters includes the project's own documented kit (MK85913, terminal SNP R-FTF47086)** — see the cross-reference gap discussed in the DNA dossier. This means the project's own genetic data currently documents a *larger, more populous* Appalachian branch of the broader Partin/Parton/Partain Y-DNA family than it does confirmed matches to the Halifax line itself, which is exactly why this material deserves its own dossier rather than being folded silently into either the Virginia or the DNA chapters.

## Identity and Name Variants

Partin / Parton / Partain / Parten — this region shows more consistent use of "Parton" and "Partain" as settled surname forms than the Virginia-to-Mississippi line, which favors "Partin."

## Documented Individuals and Family Clusters

### John Parton, b. c.1775, TN/VA — R-FTE30394 documentary anchor

Two independent FamilyTreeDNA Big Y-700 kits (public project entries 1014867 "Parton" and 1018543 "Partain") both enter the same earliest known ancestor — **John Parton, b. abt. 1775, d. aft. 1840** — and share the same terminal SNP, R-FTE30394. Two separate kit numbers converging on one documentary ancestor is a stronger internal genetic-cluster signal than a single participant's unverified tree entry, and this John Parton is the strongest candidate documentary anchor for the project's largest Appalachian DNA cluster. His exact residence, parentage, and relationship (if any) to the Virginia or Halifax Partin lines have not been researched by this project. **This is the single highest-priority research target in this dossier.**

### John Jackson "Jack" Partin, c.1780–1846, Franklin County, Tennessee

Documented in the recovered research corpus with a flagged chronological problem: public trees propose parents Charles Partin and Frances King, married 1798 — a marriage date that postdates Jack's own estimated birth (c.1780) by roughly eighteen years, an impossibility independently flagged by two separate recovered research documents. **Jack Partin's actual parentage is therefore unresolved**, and the Charles Partin/Frances King pairing should be treated as REJECTED for this specific purpose (it may describe a real marriage, but not one that could have produced a child born in 1780). No connection between this Franklin County Tennessee family and the project's Halifax line has been established.

### John Partin, Wilkes/Franklin County, Georgia, 1779–1789

Gave testimony in August 1779 in a Wilkes County, GA treason case against Absolam Thurman; received a 575-acre land grant on Big Shoal Creek, Franklin County, GA, 14 November 1788; sold the same tract with wife "Sary" (probably Sarah) to William Nicholas on 11 April 1789, the deed witnessed by George Mathews (later Governor of Georgia). Separately, a Winifred, wife of James Spratlin, relinquished dower in a 460-acre sale to John Helms in the same Franklin Co. GA record set on 13 December 1790 — a name (Winifred) that recurs suggestively but almost certainly coincidentally with the Halifax "John B" material (see `04_HALIFAX_NORTH_CAROLINA/NORTH_CAROLINA_DOSSIER.md`); no connection between this Georgia Winifred Spratlin and the Halifax Winifred Partin has been established or should be assumed. This Georgia John Partin's relationship, if any, to the Halifax line or to the Franklin Co. TN cluster above is untested.

### John Partin, Hawkins County, Tennessee, 1792/93

Sold 200 acres on the south side of the Holston River to Mashack Stacy, dated 4 December 1792 in one recovered source and 1793 in another. Geographically and chronologically distinct from the Georgia John Partin above (the Holston River valley being the Great Valley/Appalachian corridor rather than the Georgia piedmont), though the two could in principle be the same well-traveled individual — untested by this project.

### Later Appalachian-corridor sightings (untested, listed for completeness)

A John Partin, son of James and Rebecca Bozarth Partin, born 5 Mar 1818, Howard County, Missouri, married Rachel Hargrave 20 Jun 1841, Putnam Co. MO. A John Partin, "native Maury Countian" (Tennessee), married 1 Dec 1853, Greene Co. MO. A John A. Partin line traced to Bell County, Kentucky, including a John Partin born 30 Oct 1890 who married Mary Adkins 2 Jan 1935. These later Missouri/Kentucky sightings were surfaced by a single derivative narrative-synthesis document of generally low reliability (citing, among other things, at least two clearly irrelevant/mis-cited web footnotes elsewhere in the same document) and are listed here only as candidate leads for a westward Appalachian-corridor migration pattern, not as an established family reconstruction.

### Henry Carter Partain, b. 1811 — R-Y32699

Two FamilyTreeDNA Big Y-700 kits (996383 and 1003736) both enter Henry Carter Partain (b.1811, d.1868 per one) as earliest known ancestor, sharing terminal SNP R-Y32699 — the same haplogroup associated with the Anderson Co., SC cluster in the project's own raw spreadsheet. This is a second internally-consistent two-kit documentary/genetic convergence, independent of the John Parton (R-FTE30394) cluster above, and should be treated as a second distinct Appalachian-region family line, not merged with it.

### Henry Hawk Partain, b.1832, d.1917 — R-FTD95308

A single FamilyTreeDNA kit (B446750) enters this ancestor under a terminal SNP not otherwise represented in this project's data. Isolated single-kit entry; no documentary cross-reference attempted.

### Additional single-kit "Group 1" entries referenced in prior project sessions

A narrative migration-analysis document recovered in September 2026 lists four further kit/ancestor pairings, described there as part of a broader "Group 1" DNA cluster believed to validate common descent from the Jamestown-era immigrant: John Parton (b.1770, TN/NC/VA, kit 381340); a second John Parton (ca.1770s, VA/TN, kit 32356, described in that source as the "Dolly Parton line" — i.e., proposed by that document's author as sharing a common ancestor with the family of the entertainer Dolly Parton, an identification this project has not tested and does not adopt); William Partin (b.1760, VA/TN/KY, kit 175950); and Henry Partin (d.1824, AL/SC, kit 162952). **None of these four kit numbers has been cross-checked against the project's own raw 19-row spreadsheet or against the John Parton (R-FTE30394) and Henry Carter Partain (R-Y32699) clusters documented above with independent citations** — they are recorded here as leads from a single source of only moderate reliability, not as confirmed additional clusters.

## Geographic Footprint

Sevier, Cocke, Franklin, Maury, and Wilson Counties, Tennessee; Haywood County, North Carolina (western/mountain counties, not to be confused with any eastern NC county of a similar name); the Holston River valley (Hawkins Co., TN); Wilkes and Franklin Counties, Georgia; Anderson County, South Carolina; scattered later sightings in Howard and Putnam Counties, Missouri, and Bell County, Kentucky.

## DNA Evidence

See `09_DNA/YDNA_MASTER_DOSSIER.md` for the full raw dataset. In summary: this dossier's family clusters account for 13 of the 19 rows in the project's own circulated Y-DNA spreadsheet (R-FTE30394: 8 rows; R-BY31280: 2 rows; R-FTB85652: 2 rows; R-Y32699: 2 rows — one row's haplogroup is blank in the source), making the Appalachian/upland-South branch of the broader Partin/Parton/Partain Y-DNA family the best-represented branch in the project's own testing data, even though it is not the project's own documentary line. **Kit MK85913 (the project owner's own kit, terminal SNP R-FTF47086) does not appear in this spreadsheet and has not been placed relative to any of these clusters.**

## Evidence Assessment

- **STRONG (as genetic data):** The R-FTE30394 cluster's internal consistency (multiple kits, consistent TN/NC geography) and the John Parton (b. c.1775) two-kit documentary convergence; the Henry Carter Partain (b.1811) two-kit R-Y32699 convergence.
- **CANDIDATE:** Every individual documentary sighting listed above (Georgia John Partin, Hawkins Co. TN John Partin, the Missouri/Kentucky later sightings) as belonging to any specific one of the DNA clusters — none has been cross-tested.
- **REJECTED:** Charles Partin + Frances King (m.1798) as parents of John Jackson "Jack" Partin (b. c.1780) — a chronological impossibility.
- **UNTESTED, EXPLICITLY NOT ADOPTED:** Any claimed relationship to the "Dolly Parton line," and any claimed relationship between this dossier's families and the project's own documented Halifax-to-Mississippi line.

## Open Questions

1. Identify John Parton (b. c.1775, d. aft.1840)'s actual county of residence and documentary family — the strongest available anchor for the project's largest Appalachian DNA cluster.
2. Determine whether kit MK85913 (R-FTF47086) has any relationship, close or distant, to the R-FTE30394 or R-Y32699 clusters.
3. Test whether the Georgia (1779–1790) and Hawkins County, TN (1792/93) John Partin sightings represent one migratory individual or two separate men.
4. Independently verify or discard the four additional "Group 1" kit/ancestor pairings (kits 381340, 32356, 175950, 162952) against this project's own primary spreadsheet.
5. Determine whether any documentary bridge exists at all between this Appalachian cluster and the Surry/Sussex, VA generation that is this project's own documented origin — or whether the two branches diverged earlier, in England or in the earliest Virginia generations, before either line's specific ancestors are documented.

# Large Bundle 22 — Thomas Hayle Source Trial & Blackwater Proof Chain

## New findings

### 1. The Virginia source claim is substantially more specific than a mere name match
A publicly accessible transcription reproduces a General Court entry dated 4 June 1626 stating that **Thomas Hayle, age 21, was the son of Symon Hayle of the parish of St Mary Somerset in London, porter**.

This means the London hypothesis should be tested against four explicit Virginia identifiers:
- Thomas's name;
- age 21;
- father Symon;
- exact parish St Mary Somerset;
- father's occupation, porter.

The exact original/authoritative edition remains required before upgrading to PROVEN.

### 2. McCartney independently repeats the same parentage
An online excerpt from Martha W. McCartney's *Virginia Immigrants and Adventurers, 1607–1635* repeats that Thomas Hale/Hayle was the son of Symon Hayle of St Mary Somerset Parish, London, a porter, and that he arrived on the *George* in October 1623.

This is important corroboration of the transcription tradition, though it may ultimately derive from the same General Court record.

### 3. Date correction required
The indictment transcription is dated **4 June 1626**, not 1627. Margaret Partin's concealment punishment is dated **3 July 1627** and calls Thomas “lately executed.” These are distinct proceedings separated by roughly thirteen months in the surviving transcription tradition. Do not collapse them into one 1627 proceeding.

### 4. The age evidence is exceptionally tight
A London baptism candidate dated 19 May 1605 would make Thomas 21 in June 1626. The Virginia court transcription itself says age 21. This is a near-exact chronological match in addition to father name and parish.

### 5. The occupation contradiction is now the principal obstacle
Virginia: Symon = **porter**.
Recovered London will: Symon = **tailor**.

Until originals are compared, the repository must keep open:
- same man, occupational change/dual description;
- transcription error;
- two Symon Hayles in the parish;
- wrong will attached to the Virginia family.

### 6. Blackwater evidence remains a convergent chain, not yet a single proved parcel
Independent compiled land research confirms:
- 1724 Gregory & Hannah Rawlings → William Cooper, 489 acres adjoining Richard Washington and Richard Shockey;
- 1727 Robert Partin → John Justice, 200 acres Blackwater adjoining Richard Washington;
- 1754/1755 later deeds place William Cooper and John Justice as adjoining owners.

That is powerful geographic convergence but still requires the 1706 and 1727 originals to prove Robert's 200 acres are the same tract.

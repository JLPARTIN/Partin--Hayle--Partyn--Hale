# Bundle 13 — Decisive Record Strategy

Bundle 13 identifies which exact records can now change the pedigree rather than merely add context.

## Surry
The decisive record is the 1727 Elizabeth acknowledgment. If it names her as Robert's wife and attaches to the 200-acre Justice deed, Robert×Elizabeth is directly established.

The 1706 deed's consideration and boundary language can then test whether Robert's tract was a family conveyance tied to the Rawlings network.

Charles Partin's competing 1727 Rawlings-land transaction must be recovered before eliminating him as Elizabeth's husband candidate.

## Halifax
The complete 1795 probate packet—not merely the will—is the decisive family reconstruction source.

## Downstream
Barnabas's 1855 Wilkinson County probate is the best downstream anchor. Work backward from a proved Mississippi family rather than forward from copied Halifax trees.

## Land
Richard Washington's title chain is now a deliberate reconstruction tool, not a peripheral neighbor search.

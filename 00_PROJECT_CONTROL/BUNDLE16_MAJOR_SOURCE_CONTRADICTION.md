# Bundle 16 — Major Source Contradiction

A full re-read of the same Gregory Rawlings webpage reveals an internal contradiction that materially changes the Charles-vs-Robert problem.

## Version 1 — John III narrative
The page says:
- 1706 John III sells inherited land in two pieces;
- one piece goes to “his brother in law (I THINK) Charles Partin”;
- 1727 Charles sells land “late in the tenure of John Rawlings.”

## Version 2 — Elizabeth Rawlings notes on the same page
Later the same webpage says:
- Elizabeth married a PARTIN before Aug 1702;
- “May be Robert Partin who bought John's land in 1706”;
- repeats the 1706 sentence but substitutes **ROBERT PARTIN** for Charles;
- then supplies an exact abstract: Surry Deeds No.5 p.348, John Rawlings→Robert Partin, 200 acres north side Blackwater, patent of father Johnathan Rawlings 29 Apr 1692, recorded 7 May 1706;
- then again says Charles sells “that land” in 1727;
- speculates John may have overseen land “for Robert or Charles Partin.”

## Conclusion
The webpage itself does not know whether its 1706/1727 narrative belongs to Robert or Charles. Its relationship reconstruction is internally inconsistent.

**Do not use this webpage to prove a Charles 1706 deed exists.**

The only exact 1706 Partin deed citation supplied on the page is the Robert Partin deed at p.348.

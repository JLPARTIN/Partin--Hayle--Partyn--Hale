# Bundle 12 — Findings

## Major upgrade: Rawlings sibling relationship
An independent published Surry will abstract confirms that John Rawlings named:
- son John;
- son Gregory;
- daughter **Eliza. Partin**;
- wife Mary.

This means Gregory Rawlings is explicitly Elizabeth Partin's brother in the 1702/03 will.

## New implication
The 1724 Gregory Rawlings tract and Robert Partin's 1727 tract both use Richard Washington as an adjoining owner. If the 1724 Gregory is the will's Gregory, Robert is operating in the land neighborhood of Elizabeth Partin's brother.

That materially strengthens—but still does not prove—the Robert×Elizabeth Rawlings marriage hypothesis.

## Halifax caution
Search indexes indicate multiple Partin names in Halifax in the 1790s. The family must be reconstructed as separate households rather than treating every entry as John's immediate family.

## Hux caution
The Barnabas×Margaret/Peggy Hux claim remains a high-value hypothesis, but Bundle 12 explicitly tests for pedigree-copy contamination before using it as migration evidence.

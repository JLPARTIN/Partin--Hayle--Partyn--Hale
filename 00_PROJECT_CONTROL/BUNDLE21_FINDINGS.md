# Large Bundle 21 — Primary Evidence Normalization & Adjudication

## Purpose
This bundle converts the strongest recovered claims into canonical record-level evidence objects and corrects analytical overreach found in the post-recovery audit.

## Major findings
1. **Thomas Hayle's London parentage is more specific than the consolidated dossier previously reflected.** A published/secondary transcription of the Virginia General Court indictment gives Thomas as age 21 and explicitly calls him the **son of Symon Hayle of the parish of St Mary Somerset in London, porter**. This is not merely a modern name-match theory. The original/authoritative court text must still be checked.
2. **The Symon occupation problem is now a first-class contradiction.** The Virginia court transcription says **porter**; the recovered 1625/26 London will transcription says **tailor**. Do not harmonize these occupations. Possible explanations include transcription error, occupational change, two men named Symon, or a bad London-will identification.
3. **The earlier rejection of Margaret Partin = a Hayle was logically overbroad.** Symon's will omits a *daughter* Margaret, but the project's longstanding model placed Margaret as a possible **sister/relative of Symon**, not his daughter. The Hayle maiden-name hypothesis is restored to OPEN / UNPROVEN, while any specific parentage remains quarantined.
4. **The Thomas identity trial becomes sharper.** Virginia reportedly supplies father + parish + occupation; London supplies a Thomas son of Symon candidate and a Symon will naming son Thomas under 21. The next task is not broad name matching but adjudicating whether these Symons are the same man.
5. **Rawlings evidence independently confirms Eliza Partin in John Rawlings's will.** A digitized Surry wills/administrations abstract prints daughter “Eliza. Partin,” sons John and Gregory, wife Mary, made Aug 1702 and proved 25 May 1703, Book 5 p.279.
6. **The 1706 Robert deed remains the only exact Rawlings→Partin deed citation surfaced in the Rawlings compilation:** Surry Deeds No.5 p.348, John Rawlings to Robert Partin, 200 acres north side Blackwater, recorded 7 May 1706.
7. **The 1727 Robert→John Justice citation is independently recoverable in a separate compiled PDF:** Surry Deeds/Wills Etc. Book 7 Part 3 pp.726–727, 13 Mar 1727, 200 acres Blackwater adjoining Richard Washington.

## Rule adopted
Every load-bearing record should receive a canonical record note separating: source/transcription, archival locator, people, exact claims proved, claims not proved, contradictions, and next verification step.

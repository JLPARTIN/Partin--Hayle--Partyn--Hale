# Evidence Standard

This is a forensic genealogy project, not a compiled online tree.

## Claim states
- **PROVEN** — direct evidence or strong independent convergence.
- **STRONG** — substantial evidence with one material gap.
- **WORKING HYPOTHESIS** — coherent, testable reconstruction.
- **CANDIDATE** — record/person requiring identification.
- **REJECTED / UNSUPPORTED** — contradicted or inadequately supported.

## Rules
1. Original records outrank indexes and derivative trees.
2. Preserve source spellings.
3. Never merge same-name people without an identity argument.
4. Separate transcription, interpretation and inference.
5. Log negative searches.
6. DNA corroborates patrilineal structure but does not independently prove a named ancestor.
7. Preserve contradictions rather than harmonizing them away.
8. State what would falsify or strengthen each major conclusion.

## Required dossier structure
Proposition; known facts; chronology; source-by-source evidence; FAN network; competing explanations; contradictions; assessment; unresolved questions; archival targets; source notes.

# Bundle 23 — Archival Targets

## 1. 1700 Surry tithables
Recover the original/authoritative 1700 list containing Edward Scarborough and Robert Partin. Capture adjacent entries and list structure.

## 2. Scarborough FAN reconstruction
Map Edward Scarborough's land and court activity 1695–1703. Search Robert Partin as witness, debtor, servant, tenant or co-resident.

## 3. Virginia Patent Book 5
Verify Partin-type headrights:
- Samuel;
- Mary;
- William;
- Edward;
- Francis.

Determine whether repeated entries are duplicate headright claims or distinct people.

## 4. 1662 Samuel patent
Obtain Land Patent Book 5 page corresponding to Dennis Eyes/Conway patent and inspect exact surname.

## 5. 1697–1706 Surry Partin sweep
Search tax, court order, deed, probate and parish material for every Part*n spelling. The 1700 lead suggests records before the 1706 deed may exist.

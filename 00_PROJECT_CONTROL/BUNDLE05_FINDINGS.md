# Bundle 05 — Findings and Direction

## Finding 1 — 1637 lease upgraded
The Robert Partin 40-acre Elizabeth City lease now has full published abstract details and independent geographic corroboration. It is no longer merely an index target.

## Finding 2 — 1648 family cluster corroborated
The Seward patent/headright list is independently reproduced by more than one compilation and names Robert, Margaret, Robert Jr., Avis, Rebecca and Deborah.

## Finding 3 — Rawlings connection is familial
The 1702/03 John Rawlings will names a daughter **Eliza. Partin**. This changes interpretation of the 1706 Rawlings→Robert Partin deed: a Partin marriage into the Rawlings family existed before the conveyance.

## Finding 4 — Hux may be a migration-network marker
William Partin and Elizabeth Hux appear together as witnesses in a 1737 Surry abstract; a Hux→Partin land transaction appears later in the Halifax research trail. This deserves full reconstruction.

## Current highest-value question
**Who was Eliza. Partin, daughter of John Rawlings?**

Solving that marriage could identify the generation occupying the 1706 tract and materially tighten the 1655→1706 bridge.

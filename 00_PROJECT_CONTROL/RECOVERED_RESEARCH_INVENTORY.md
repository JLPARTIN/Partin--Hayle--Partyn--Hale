# Recovered Research Inventory

This inventory catalogs the AI-assisted genealogical research corpus recovered from the project owner's Google Drive during the September 2026 recovery pass. It exists so that no scattered file is lost and so downstream dossiers can cite a canonical source rather than re-describing duplicates. Local machine paths, account identifiers, and personal/unrelated files have been excluded or sanitized per the recovery brief.

**Recovery method:** Full-text and title search of the connected Google Drive account for the surnames Partin/Partyn/Parton/Partain/Hayle/Hale and associated place names, followed by systematic reading of every matched document via parallel research agents. See `RESEARCH_RECOVERY_LOG.md` for the search methodology and what was and was not covered.

**Scale found:** ~110 distinct Drive items matched the search; after deduplication (many identical or near-identical re-saves from repeated AI chat sessions), this resolves to approximately **62 unique research documents/data sets**, most produced through AI-assisted (ChatGPT/Gemini/other LLM) research sessions run by the project owner between January and April 2026, plus **one externally authored book-length secondary source** (the "Barnabas Partin" register by Jim Partin), **two Y-DNA data spreadsheets**, and **one unread DNA-related video file**.

**Critical framing note:** With the sole exception of the Jim Partin book and the Y-DNA spreadsheets, none of this corpus is itself a primary source. It is AI-assisted secondary research — much of it citing primary records with real archival identifiers (FamilySearch ARK/DGS/image numbers, deed/will book-and-page citations), but none of it independently confirmed as image-reviewed by the documents' own admission. Every citation below should be read as "a lead to verify," not "a fact." Where a document explicitly self-labels its confidence (CONFIRMED, hypothesis, IDENTITY UNRESOLVED, Not-Image-Viewed, etc.), that label is preserved.

## Category G — The Jim Partin Book: *Barnabas Partin: Ancestors and Descendants, From Virginia to Mississippi and Beyond*

**This is the single most important source in the recovered corpus and the only externally authored, book-length secondary source found.** Compiled by **Jim Partin** (Jimmy Dail Partin), P.O. Box 97, Hamilton, MS 39746, described internally as "six years work"; reaches descendants born into the 1980s, so it was compiled/updated across the late 20th century. It uses genealogical Register/NGSQ-style numbering, structured biographical fields, and a numbered "Research:" source list per person citing real deed/will/tax/census/newspaper records.

**Canonical copy** (the original, full-resolution scan — treat as authoritative): a 7-file set in one Drive folder —
- "[1] BARNABAS PARTIN - COVER.jpg"
- "[2] BARNABAS PARTIN 1-100.pdf" (87.5 MB)
- "[3] BARNBAS PARTIN 101-200.pdf" (71.9 MB)
- "[4] BARNBAS PARTIN 201-300.pdf" (72.9 MB)
- "[5] BARNABAS PARTIN 301-400.pdf" (73.3 MB)
- "[6] BARNABAS PARTIN 401-500.pdf" (80.4 MB)
- "[7] BARNABAS PARTIN 501-588.pdf" (80.6 MB)

**Duplicate status — do not re-process these as separate sources:** the same 588 pages exist again as OCR'd Google Docs under two different naming conventions ("[1-100][PARTIN][DOCX]"…"[501-588][PARTIN][DOCX]" AND "[1]BARNABAS_PARTIN[1]"…"[6]BARNABAS_PARTIN_501-588-1"), each re-OCR'd **three to five times** at slightly different byte sizes (confirmed by direct character-comparison: two of the "1-100" copies are identical except for one stray OCR character), plus compressed PDF re-saves, RTF exports, a zip file, a shortcut, two duplicate folder copies of the whole set, "Partin Tree 01-28.pdf" (confirmed to be a third, cleaner-OCR scan of the book's opening chapters that preserves the original printed page numbers), and one unexplained 178 MB "BARNABAS PARTIN (3).pdf" whose relationship to the canonical set is unconfirmed. None of these ~25 duplicate items should be catalogued or cited separately; every citation to this book should reference the canonical 7-file set above.

**Content recovered (via two independent sampling passes):**
- ~1,090+ person entries across the six page-range files; Files 1 and 5 contain the direct Robert Partin/Barnabas Partin lineage material (Robert Partin Sr./Margaret Hayle/Thomas Hayle immigrant entry with 1619–1655 VA land patents; a Surry Co. VA Robert Partin/Elizabeth Rawlings entry with full will/estate-inventory text; John Partin's 1795 Halifax will naming sons Robert, Brasel, Barnabas, David, Charles; the full Barnabas Partin/Margaret "Peggy" Hux entry with an 1819 federal land patent and Wilkinson Co. MS deed/probate citations). Files 2–4 (Louisiana/Mississippi collateral descendants, 20th century) contain no hits on the project's core target individuals.
- Real citations throughout: Surry Co. VA Deed/Will Books 5–9, Halifax Co. NC Deed Books 9–14 and Will Book 3, Wilkinson Co. MS Land Conveyance Books K/L/M/Q/S/T/U/UU/VV/YY/3K, Wilkinson Co. MS Probate Wills Book 2 and Probate Minutes Vol. 9.
- The book's own author flags several of its own claims as unconfirmed (Thomas Partin's parentage: "I do not have confirmed proof that Thomas was a son of Barnabas"; Asa B. Partin as Brasel's son: "this must be proven") — these hedges are preserved in the dossiers rather than silently resolved.
- One File 6 tail (the last ~87 of the ~588 pages, roughly persons 500–588) was not fully retrievable in this pass due to tool output limits — flagged in `RESEARCH_RECOVERY_LOG.md` as unfinished.

**Copyright note:** this is a copyrighted, individually authored work. Per project practice, it is cited and its factual content (names, dates, relationships, the primary-record citations it reproduces) is summarized into the dossiers with attribution; its original narrative prose is not reproduced at length in this repository.

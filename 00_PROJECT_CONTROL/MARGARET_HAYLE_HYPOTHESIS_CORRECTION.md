# Correction — Margaret Partin / Hayle Hypothesis

## Problem found in post-recovery audit
The recovery pass rejected “Margaret Partin = Margaret Hayle” because Symon Hayle's 1625/26 will does not name a daughter Margaret.

That reasoning tested the wrong relationship.

The project's older working model placed Margaret as a possible **sister/relative of Symon**, potentially making Symon's son Thomas her nephew. A will's list of Symon's children cannot by itself exclude a sister of Symon.

## Correct status
- **Margaret Partin's maiden name = Hayle:** OPEN / UNPROVEN.
- **Margaret was a daughter of Symon Hayle:** unsupported by the recovered will.
- **Margaret was a sister/other relative of Symon:** WORKING HYPOTHESIS requiring independent evidence.
- **Specific unsourced online parent assignments:** QUARANTINED.

## Required proof
Reconstruct Symon's parents and siblings from original parish/probate/apprenticeship records; identify Margaret independently before Virginia; then test whether the Virginia Margaret fits that London person chronologically and geographically.

## Rule
Do not convert negative evidence against one relationship category into rejection of every possible relationship category.

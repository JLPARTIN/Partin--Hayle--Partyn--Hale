# Bundle 14 — Findings

## Major correction / breakthrough
The Charles Partin model is substantially stronger than previously understood.

The Rawlings research compilation explicitly says that in 1706 John Rawlings III sold inherited land in two pieces, **one to his brother-in-law Charles Partin**. It also reports Charles selling that land in 1727 as land “late in the tenure of John Rawlings.”

This does not yet prove Charles married Elizabeth Rawlings because “brother in law” may be the compiler's inference. But it means the project must stop treating Robert as the near-default husband.

## New decisive task
Recover both 1706 Partin conveyances and both 1727 Partin conveyances. The identity of Elizabeth's husband may be resolved by wife/dower language and parcel chains.

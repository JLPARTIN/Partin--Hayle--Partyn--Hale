# Bundle 18 — Findings

## New documentary leverage: John Justice tract
An independent Surry land reconstruction supplies a powerful later-neighbor test for Robert Partin's 1727 conveyance.

Robert Partin sold John Justice 200 acres on Blackwater Swamp on 13 Mar 1727, adjoining Richard Washington.

Later deeds show land associated with John Justice in the same neighborhood adjoining William Cooper. William Cooper had acquired 489 acres from Gregory and Hannah Rawlings in 1724, adjoining Richard Washington and Richard Shockey.

This does not yet prove Robert's 200 acres was the 1706 Rawlings tract, but it creates a multi-record geographic chain:
**Gregory Rawlings → William Cooper ↔ John Justice ← Robert Partin**, with Richard Washington as a shared fixed boundary.

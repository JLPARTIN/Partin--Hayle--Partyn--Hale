# Jamestown → Surry Identity Models After the 1700 Tax Lead

## Anchor A
1655: Robert Partin Senior + Robert Partin Junior associated with Shirley Hundred land.

## Anchor B
1657: a Robert Partin in Elizabeth City tenure with Gresham Covell; exact Robert unresolved.

## Anchor C
1700: derivative Surry tax transcription reports Edward Scarborough + Robert Partin, two polls.

## Anchor D
1706: John Rawlings → Robert Partin, 200 acres Blackwater.

## Models

### Model 1 — 1655 Jr. survives to become 1700/1706 Robert
Chronologically possible but would make the 1624/25 infant about 75 in 1700 and 81 in 1706.

### Model 2 — 1700/1706 Robert is son of 1655 Jr.
Chronologically natural, but no recovered parentage record currently proves it.

### Model 3 — collateral descendant of the Jamestown family
Possible; requires an undocumented branch.

### Model 4 — unrelated Virginia Partin line
Must remain open because multiple Partin-named headrights occur in Virginia during 1662–1683.

## Research implication
The new surname-control audit increases the burden of proof. Model 2 may be attractive chronologically, but cannot be promoted simply because online pedigrees insert an intermediate Robert.

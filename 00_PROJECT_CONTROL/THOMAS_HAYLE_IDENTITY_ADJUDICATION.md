# Thomas Hayle Identity Adjudication — London ↔ Virginia

## Question
Is the Thomas Hayle in Robert Partin's Virginia household the same Thomas who belonged to the St Mary Somerset family of Symon Hayle in London?

## Evidence matrix

| Evidence | Observation | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Virginia muster | Thomas Hale/Hayle, servant in Partin household, about age 20; arrived 1623 | strong identity anchor |
| Virginia court transcription | Thomas age 21; explicitly son of Symon Hayle; St Mary Somerset, London; father described as porter | potentially decisive if authoritative wording verified |
| London parish candidate | Thomas Hayle, son of Symon, baptism candidate 19 May 1605, St Mary Somerset | strong chronological/parish fit, original needed |
| Symon will | St Mary Somerset Symon names son Thomas under 21 in Feb 1625/26 | strong chronological/familial fit, original needed |
| Occupation | court: porter; will: tailor | material contradiction |
| Margaret theory | possible Hayle relative of Symon | open; not needed to establish Thomas's parentage |

## Current adjudication
**STRONG CANDIDATE / near-identity convergence, not yet PROVEN.** Father name + exact parish + compatible age is far stronger than a same-name match. The occupation contradiction prevents automatic merger until the authoritative court and probate records are compared.

## Falsification paths
- London Thomas survives in England after the Virginia Thomas's execution.
- Court father/parish wording proves to be a derivative embellishment rather than original wording.
- Probate/parish records show the will's Symon and the court's Symon are demonstrably different men.
- The 1605 baptism belongs to a different Symon household.

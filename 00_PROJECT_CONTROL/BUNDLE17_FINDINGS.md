# Bundle 17 — Findings

## Major Rawlings clarification
A fresh source comparison separates **two different Elizabeth Rawlings women** in late-17th-century Surry research.

The Elizabeth who married William Petway and then John Davis belongs to the **Roger & Alice (Newsom) Rawlings** line. She is not automatically the Eliza Partin named in John Rawlings's 1702/03 will.

The Eliza Partin in the 1702/03 will is explicitly the daughter of that testator John Rawlings and sister of John and Gregory.

## 1697 Anna Jordan lead
The 1697 will abstract names both John Rawlings and Elizabeth Rawlings and has Mary Rawlings as a witness. The derivative compiler assigns these to the family of John d.1702/03, but this identification still needs the original and network context.

## Methodological upgrade
The repository now quarantines same-name Elizabeth Rawlings identities instead of merging them.

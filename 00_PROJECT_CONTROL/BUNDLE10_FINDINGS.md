# Bundle 10 — New Findings

## 1. Blackwater neighborhood is becoming reconstructable
Robert Partin's 1727 tract shares Richard Washington as a boundary marker with a 1724 Gregory Rawlings transaction. Later deeds keep John Justice and William Cooper in the same neighborhood. This gives us a concrete parcel/FAN cluster rather than isolated surname hits.

## 2. David Partin is independently documented in Halifax after John's death
A 1797 tax abstract places David Partin, one free poll, in Halifax District 2. This independently corroborates the old research tradition that a David belonged to the late-1790s Halifax Partin family, though relationship to John still requires probate.

## 3. Barnabas now has a live Big Y anchor
FTDNA public project results show a Partin Big Y-700 kit, MK85913, entered with earliest ancestor Barnaby Partin b.1773 d.1855 and haplogroup R-FTF47086.

## 4. Hux hypothesis becomes more consequential
Modern trees repeatedly call Barnabas's wife Margaret/Peggy Hux. It remains unproved, but if verified would extend the Hux association across another Partin generation.

## Next breakthrough targets
- prove Gregory Rawlings relationship to John Rawlings;
- recover 1795 probate and test David;
- recover Barnabas×Hux marriage/probate evidence;
- map R-FTF47086 against other Partin branches.

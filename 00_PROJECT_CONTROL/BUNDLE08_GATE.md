# Bundle 08 — Halifax Consolidation Gate

## Two separate proofs
A. Prove the Halifax John internally across 1762–1795.
B. Then prove or disprove his identity with the Virginia John.

Keeping these separate prevents circular reasoning.

## Immediate documentary bullseye
The 1767 Hux deed can establish John's land neighborhood and perhaps residence/origin. The 1795 probate can establish his family. Together they frame the Halifax man.

## Downstream gate
Once Barnabas is explicitly tied to John, the research can move generation-by-generation through the Carolinas to Mississippi while DNA tests the broader paternal branching model.

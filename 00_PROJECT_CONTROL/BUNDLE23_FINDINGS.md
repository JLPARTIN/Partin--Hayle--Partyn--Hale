# Large Bundle 23 — Earliest Surry Robert & Virginia Surname-Control Audit

## Breakthrough 1 — Robert Partin appears in a reported 1700 Surry tithable/tax entry
A genealogical transcription of Edward Scarborough's Surry tax sequence reports:

**1700 Surry County tax list: Edward Scarborough & Robt Partin — 2 polls.**

The same sequence shows Scarborough alone with one poll in 1695, 1696, 1698, 1699, 1701, 1702 and 1703.

### Significance
If the tax transcription is accurate, Robert Partin was physically/taxably associated with Edward Scarborough in Surry by **1700** — six years before the known 1706 Rawlings→Robert deed and two years before John Rawlings wrote the will naming daughter Eliza Partin.

This becomes the earliest currently surfaced Surry anchor for Robert in this phase of the project.

### Caution
The transcription does not state the relationship between Scarborough and Partin. A combined poll entry can reflect household/employment/residence/tax accounting and is not kinship proof. The original tithable list is required.

## Breakthrough 2 — the surname was not unique to Robert's Virginia family
A compiled Partin research PDF reproduces Virginia Land Patent Book 5/6/7 headright entries for multiple Partin-named people in the 1660s–1680s:
- Samll. Partin — 1662;
- Mary Partin — 1663/1664;
- Wm. Partin — 1663;
- Edw. Partin — 1663;
- Francis Partin — 1665;
- Jane Pantin/Partin — 1672;
- Mary Partan — 1683.

At least the Samuel entry is independently reproduced in another transcription of the 11 Mar 1662 Northumberland patent.

### Consequence
We must stop treating the surname Partin in later Virginia as automatically identifying a descendant of Robert of Jamestown. The 1655→1700/Surry bridge must be proved genealogically, not assumed from surname rarity.

## Breakthrough 3 — 1700 narrows the Surry bridge
The prior bridge was framed broadly as 1655/57→1706. If the 1700 tax entry is verified, the immediate Surry problem becomes:
**Who is the Robert Partin taxed with Edward Scarborough in 1700, and how does he relate to the 1655 Robert Sr./Jr. pair?**

That is a much sharper identity trial.

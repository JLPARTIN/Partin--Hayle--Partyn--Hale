# William Partin → John + Winifred Parentage Trial

## Hypothesis
The William Partin active in the Halifax cluster may have been father of John B (wife Winifred).

## Evidence favoring investigation
- William belongs to the same Halifax Partin neighborhood.
- Earlier research places William around Hunter's Mill Swamp / John Partin land.
- William appears in the elder John's 1795 witness/FAN network.
- 14 Feb 1796 reported William purchase: 80 acres south side Hunter's Mill Swamp, beginning on John Parten's line / near John Partine plantation.
- A William Partin was already part of the wider Virginia→NC corridor in earlier decades.

## Problems
No recovered record explicitly calls John B “son of William.”
The 1795 elder-John will may or may not name a son John; project notes conflict.
There may be more than one William.

## Tests
1. Recover William's probate and identify heirs.
2. Trace William's 1796 80 acres backward and forward.
3. Compare William's land to John B's 1793 March Swamp tract.
4. Search for deeds of gift or nominal-consideration transfers.
5. Compare John B's witnesses/associates with William's.
6. Search Kershaw for William-associated FAN surnames.

## Status
**WORKING HYPOTHESIS — unproved.**

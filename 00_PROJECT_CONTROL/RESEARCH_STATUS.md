# Research Status

## Active thesis
The project is testing whether a Thames-side London Partyn/Hayle network explains the early Virginia Partin–Hayle association and whether that colonial population can be traced through later Surry/Sussex and Halifax lines into the Carolinas and Mississippi.

## Highest-value current propositions
1. Virginia Robert Partin = London Robert Partyn baptized 1 Jun 1589.
2. Margaret Partin = Margaret Hayle of the reconstructed London family.
3. Virginia Thomas Hayle = Thomas baptized 19 May 1605 at St Mary Somerset.
4. Thomas's presence with Robert reflects kinship through Margaret.
5. The Hayle network may explain migration access/opportunity.
6. Early and later Virginia Partins form one patrilineal family, but the exact generation chain must be demonstrated.
7. Halifax John derives from the Surry/Sussex cluster; exact parentage remains under adjudication.
8. Y-DNA branch structure can help distinguish competing documentary models.

## Things not to state as fact
- Robert was a poor Bristol/Somerset indentured servant.
- “Somerset” in the relevant London material means Somerset county.
- Margaret Hayle was Robert's wife absent a proof argument.
- Any same-name John/Robert is automatically the next generation.
- FTDNA mutation dates identify a specific colonial man without documentary correlation.

# Large Bundle 24 — John + Winifred Branch Reconstruction

## Purpose
Recover and formalize the older research on the separate Halifax John Partin whose wife was Winifred, then test his relationship to the elder John (d.1795), Barnabas, and William.

## Identity separation
**John A:** elder Halifax John, wife Mary, died 1795.
**John B:** Halifax John whose wife Winifred released dower in the 1793 March Swamp deed and again in South Carolina in 1802.

These men cannot be the same husband because John A's wife Mary survives in his probate context while John B is demonstrably paired with Winifred.

## Strong migration finding
John B + Winifred sold Halifax land in 1793. A John Partin + Winifred then held/sold a 150-acre Pine Tree Creek tract in Kershaw District, SC by 1800–1802. Winifred's repeated dower role is the strongest identity bridge.

Barnabas appears in the Kershaw/Claremont-Sumter corridor in 1801. The near-simultaneous movement supports a shared family/FAN migration, though it does not by itself prove siblinghood.

## William question
Earlier work identified William Partin in the elder John's Halifax neighborhood and as a witness in the 1795 probate network. The older hypothesis that William may be John B's father remains viable but unproved.

The competing hypothesis that John B is a son/devisee of John A must be tested against the original 1795 will, because recovered notes conflict over whether a son John was actually named.

## Critical correction
Do not state that John B was proved to be a son of John A until the original will is re-read. Earlier project summaries conflict: some list no son John; other recovered repository notes say the will named John. This contradiction is now an explicit proof gate.

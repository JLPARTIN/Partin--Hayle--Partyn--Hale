# John & Winifred Partin — Kershaw Cluster

## Pine Tree Creek
Recovered project research places John + Winifred on a 150-acre Pine Tree Creek tract by 1800 and documents its sale on 24 Feb 1802, followed by Winifred's dower acknowledgment on 4 Mar 1802.

Reported boundary names include John Burchett and Joseph Alexander. Reported witnesses/readings require image verification.

## Barnabas overlap
Barnabas Partin appears in the same broader Kershaw/Claremont migration corridor:
- 20 Jan 1801: witness/deponent in a Claremont deed involving Samuel Payne/Paym;
- 21 Jan 1801: reported purchase of two Kershaw tracts from Samuel Payne.

This puts Barnabas and John B in South Carolina at nearly the same time after John B's 1793 Halifax land sale.

## Interpretation
The chronology is consistent with a coordinated extended-family migration from Halifax into the Kershaw/Claremont region.

It is not sufficient alone to choose between brother, nephew/cousin, or another close relationship.

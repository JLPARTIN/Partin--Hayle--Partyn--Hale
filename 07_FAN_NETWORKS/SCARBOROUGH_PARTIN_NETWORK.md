# Scarborough–Partin FAN Network

## 1700 anchor
Edward Scarborough and Robert Partin reportedly appear together for two polls on a Surry tax list.

## Why Edward matters
Scarborough is independently traceable in Surry tax lists before and after 1700, making him a fixed FAN person rather than a one-off surname.

## Research questions
1. Where was Edward Scarborough's land in 1700 relative to Blackwater and the Rawlings/Nicholson network?
2. Who were Scarborough's neighbors, witnesses, debtors and creditors?
3. Does Robert appear in a court/deed record as servant, tenant, witness or debtor of Scarborough?
4. Does Scarborough connect to Robert Nicholson/Hannah Nicholson/Sampson Lanier, witnesses to John Rawlings's will?
5. Is the 1700 Robert the same man who buys Rawlings land in 1706?

## Important negative pattern
The derivative sequence reports Scarborough alone in 1699 and 1701. Robert's one-year co-listing may mark a short residence/employment episode rather than ownership.

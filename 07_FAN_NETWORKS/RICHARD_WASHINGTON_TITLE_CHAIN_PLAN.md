# Richard Washington — Title Chain Plan

Richard Washington is the shared boundary marker linking the 1724 Gregory Rawlings and 1727 Robert Partin land evidence.

## Search window
1690–1740 Surry deed/patent records.

## Extract
- every tract on/near Blackwater;
- prior owner;
- acreage;
- boundary neighbors;
- creek/swamp/branch names;
- later grantee;
- witnesses.

## Objective
Use Washington's land as the fixed edge against which the Rawlings and Partin parcels can be reconstructed.

A neighbor's title chain may explicitly identify a boundary as “Partin's,” “Rawlings's,” or a former owner's line and thereby bridge records that do not reference one another directly.

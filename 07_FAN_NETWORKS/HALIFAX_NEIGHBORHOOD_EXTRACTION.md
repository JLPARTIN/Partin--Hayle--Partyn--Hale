# Halifax Neighborhood Extraction Protocol

For each John Partin record from 1762–1795, extract all surrounding names.

## Sources
- tax lists: adjacent taxpayers / district captain;
- deeds: grantors, grantees, witnesses, adjoining owners;
- road orders: overseer and road hands;
- probate: witnesses, securities, appraisers, buyers;
- court minutes: co-defendants, petitioners, jurors.

## Output
Create a frequency table of associates. Names appearing in three or more independent record types become priority FAN candidates.

## Cross-state test
Search high-frequency Halifax associates backward in Virginia and forward with Barnabas into South Carolina/Mississippi.

# Brasseur / Brasheare — Partin Network

## 1637 anchor
Robert Partin's Elizabeth City 40-acre lease was bounded on the west by **Robert Brasheare**.

## Why follow him
Brasseur/Brashere family research places Robert Brasseur in the colony by 1637 and later in Upper Norfolk/Nansemond. That movement may help reconstruct the geographic corridor around Partin's Elizabeth City property.

## Research tasks
- recover Robert Brasseur patents 1638/39 onward;
- map Partin 1637 lease against Fort Field/church;
- determine whether any Brasseur, Thompson or church/glebe references recur with Partins;
- do not infer kinship from adjacency alone.

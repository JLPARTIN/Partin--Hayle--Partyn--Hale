# Shirley Hundred FAN Network — 1620s–1655

## Core Partin household
Robert Partin Sr.; Margaret Partin; children Avis, Rebecca, Robert Jr.; servant Thomas Hale; servant Ellin Cooke.

## 1655 land-chain associates
The Daniel Llewellyn patent abstract places the Partin land transaction amid a dense local network:
- Daniel Llewellyn / Luellin
- John Meares
- Walter Aston
- Edward Gardner
- Edward Madison / Maddin
- Sergeant John Harris
- Michael Turpin
- Joseph Royall
- Dorothy Baker / John Baker

## Research use
Search these people in deeds, patents, wills and court minutes for:
- Partin witnesses;
- adjoining tracts;
- prior conveyances;
- estate debts;
- marriage/guardianship connections;
- references to Robert Sr. versus Jr.

The goal is to reconstruct the **land neighborhood**, not merely collect Partin surname hits. A surviving deed in another family's papers may preserve a Partin relationship lost from the Partin record series.

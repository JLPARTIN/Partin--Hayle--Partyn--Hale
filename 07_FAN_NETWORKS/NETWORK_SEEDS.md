- Ellin Cooke — servant in same household.
- Richard Domelawe/Domelow — Robert reportedly debtor in 1620.
- Alexander Winchelsey — Robert reportedly mentioned in will context.

## Surry

## Halifax / Edgecombe
- William and Ann Hux — reported 1767 grantors.
- Elias Fort — 1772-associated research target.
- Road-order participants in 1774.
- Probate witnesses, executors, securities and purchasers in 1795.

## Method
For every associate create a dated event link, not a vague “associated surname.” Repeated links across jurisdictions receive priority because they can distinguish migration from same-name coincidence.

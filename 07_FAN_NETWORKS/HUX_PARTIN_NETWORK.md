# Hux–Partin Network

## Surry seed
A 1737 Surry will abstract contains witnesses **William Partin** and **Elizabeth Hux**.

## Halifax seed
Older research reports an 11 Aug 1767 Halifax deed from **William and Ann Hux** to **John Partin**, 100 acres adjoining Great Darlling Swamp.

## Hypothesis
The Hux surname may represent a FAN connection carried from the Surry/Sussex environment into Halifax.

## Required test
Reconstruct Hux households in Surry/Sussex and Halifax/Edgecombe from 1720–1770. Identify Elizabeth Hux, William Hux and Ann Hux independently, then test migration and kinship.

This could become a useful bridge marker because uncommon recurring associates are often more discriminating than the Partin surname itself.

# Justice–Cooper–Rawlings FAN Network

The land evidence now gives a concrete FAN structure rather than a generic neighbor list.

## Nodes
- Gregory Rawlings — Elizabeth Partin's documented brother.
- William Cooper — Gregory's 1724 grantee.
- Robert Partin — John Rawlings's 1706 grantee.
- John Justice — Robert's 1727 grantee.
- Richard Washington — common boundary anchor.
- Richard Shockey — 1724 boundary.
- John Nicholson / Benjamin Ricker — later neighborhood.

## Key relationship
Later deeds place **John Justice and William Cooper as adjoining landowners**.

That means Robert's grantee and Gregory's grantee occupy the same reconstructed neighborhood.

## Research value
Witnesses, probate, road orders and later deeds for Justice/Cooper may preserve Partin or Rawlings references after the original conveyances.

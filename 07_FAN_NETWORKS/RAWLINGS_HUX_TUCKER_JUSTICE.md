# Rawlings–Hux–Tucker–Justice FAN Complex

## Rawlings
Strongest family-network evidence: John Rawlings names daughter Eliza. Partin by 1702/03; conveys land to Robert in 1706.

## Tucker
Robert and Charles reportedly occur in Tucker estate material 1721–22. Recover exact roles: debtor, creditor, witness, appraiser, purchaser, etc.

## Justice
John Justice reportedly buys Robert's 200-acre tract in March 1727. Determine whether this is the Rawlings tract and whether Justice reappears with Partins.

## Hux
William Partin and Elizabeth Hux co-occur as witnesses in 1737 Surry material; William/Ann Hux later reportedly convey Halifax land to John Partin in 1767.

## Research proposition
A repeated associate chain across Surry/Sussex and Halifax may be more probative of migration than a bare same-name John Partin match.

## Required discipline
Each FAN link must be dated, sourced and assigned a relationship type. No surname is treated as hereditary evidence by itself.

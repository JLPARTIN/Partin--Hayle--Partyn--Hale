# Y-DNA — Halifax / Sussex Branch Test

## Working observation
The project reports that Halifax and Sussex remained distinct while several other Group 1 lines were reorganized beneath newer FTT183/FTT184/FTT185 structure.

## Documentary question
Do those genetic branches correspond to early splits within the colonial Virginia Partin population?

## Test design
For every Big Y tester:
- terminal SNP;
- immediate upstream SNP;
- earliest proved paternal ancestor;
- earliest county/state;
- documentary confidence;
- mutation-age interval with retrieval date.

Then compare the genetic branching order to documentary branch points.

## Critical restraint
A SNP date cannot identify “Robert Partin Jr.” or another named colonial man by itself. DNA can constrain branch order and relatedness; genealogy must identify the historical individuals.

## High-value prediction
If Halifax and Sussex are genuinely early-diverging branches, their common upstream node should predate the downstream SNPs shared by the newly merged lines. This is testable against the live FTDNA tree.

# Halifax vs Sussex — Testable Y-DNA Predictions

The historical hypothesis should make predictions before the tree is interpreted.

## If Halifax and Sussex are early colonial splits
We expect:
- distinct downstream terminal SNPs;
- a shared upstream Partin node;
- divergence older than mutations uniting later merged Group 1 lines.

## If one descends from the other in a later period
Their branching topology should be nested rather than parallel.

## If documentary assignments are wrong
A tester may cluster genetically with a different historical branch than the pedigree label predicts.

## Guardrail
Do not move a historical ancestor merely to make the paper tree fit a SNP topology. Flag the conflict and re-audit both evidence streams.

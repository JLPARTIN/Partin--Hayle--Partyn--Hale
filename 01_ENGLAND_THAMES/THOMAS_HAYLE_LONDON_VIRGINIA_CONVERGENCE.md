# Thomas Hayle — London/Virginia Convergence

## Virginia identifiers
The reported 4 June 1626 court entry supplies an unusually rich identity signature:
- Thomas Hayle;
- age 21;
- father Symon Hayle;
- St Mary Somerset, London;
- father described as porter.

## London candidate
The project's London research has a Thomas Hayle baptism candidate:
- 19 May 1605;
- son of Symon;
- St Mary Somerset.

A 19 May 1605 baptism produces age 21 on 4 June 1626 almost exactly.

## Symon will candidate
The recovered 10 Feb 1625/26 will for a Symon Hayle of St Mary Somerset names a son Thomas who had not yet reached 21. That too is chronologically compatible with a man turning 21 in spring 1626.

## Combined evidentiary force
Name alone would be weak.
Name + father + exact parish + exact age is a much narrower identity signature.

The unresolved occupation conflict prevents PROVEN status:
- court tradition: porter;
- will transcription: tailor.

## Working adjudication
**STRONG CANDIDATE for same Thomas.**

The next investigation should reconstruct every Symon Hayle in St Mary Somerset c.1595–1626 and determine whether porter and tailor can belong to one man.

# Symon Hayle — Porter vs Tailor Identity Trial

## Evidence A — Virginia court tradition
Thomas Hayle's father is described as **Symon Hayle of St Mary Somerset, London, porter**.

## Evidence B — recovered London probate transcription
A Symon Hayle of St Mary Somerset is described as **tailor** and names a son Thomas under 21.

## Competing models

### Model A — same man, two occupational descriptions
Possible, but requires evidence. A person might change trade or hold an office in addition to a trade. Do not assume.

### Model B — transcription/reading error
“porter” or “tailor” may have been misread or modernized incorrectly. Originals decide.

### Model C — two Symon Hayles
Two men of the same name may have lived in or been associated with the parish. Parish, subsidy, livery and probate reconstruction can test this.

### Model D — wrong will
The recovered will may concern a different Symon than Thomas's father despite parish/name overlap.

## Search program
- original will;
- General Court authoritative text;
- London apprenticeship registers;
- freedom/livery records;
- parish vestry/churchwarden material;
- subsidy/tax lists;
- burial record;
- probate witnesses;
- occupational directories/indexes where available.

## Rule
Do not merge the two Symons until the occupation contradiction is explained by evidence.

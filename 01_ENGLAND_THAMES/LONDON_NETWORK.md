# London / Thames Network — Partyn and Hayle

## Research purpose
Reconstruct the English social network behind Robert Partin and Thomas Hayle rather than searching surnames in isolation.

## Partyn/Partin candidates
Current project work includes:
- John Parton baptized 4 Jul 1568 at St Botolph Aldgate, son of Peter — candidate only.
- John Partin/Partyn and Alice Gaskyn marriage candidate, 1586.
- Robert Partyn baptism candidate, 1 Jun 1589.

The 1589 date is important because the Virginia muster age for Robert points approximately to 1588/89. Chronological agreement is evidence of compatibility, not identity.

## Hayle candidates
The reconstructed cluster includes John Hayle, Symon Hayle, Margaret Hayle, Symon's wife Margaret Clement, and Thomas Hayle. Research centers on St Mary Somerset and associated London records.

Candidate chronology:
- Symon Hayle baptism: 3 Mar 1576.
- Margaret Hayle baptism: 25 Mar 1588.
- Symon Hayle–Margaret Clement marriage: 1601.
- Thomas Hayle baptism: 19 May 1605.
- Symon burial candidate: 28 Mar 1626.

## St Mary Somerset correction
“St Mary Somerset” is a London parish name. A record attached to that parish cannot be converted merely from the word *Somerset* into evidence that the person came from Somerset county or Bristol. Any Bristol/Somerset origin theory requires independent evidence.

## Network hypothesis
If Margaret Hayle became Margaret Partin, Thomas would be her nephew under the current sibling reconstruction. Thomas later appearing in Robert Partin's Virginia household would then have a direct kinship explanation.

That is a powerful hypothesis because it predicts additional evidence: probate kinship language, shared witnesses, parish overlap, apprenticeship/livery ties, occupational connections, or migration sponsorship. It remains a hypothesis until those predictions are tested.

## Archival program
Recover originals for all candidate baptisms/marriages/burials; reconstruct every sibling; obtain John Hayle probate; search apprenticeships, freemen/livery records, subsidy/tax records, ecclesiastical material, Thames occupations, shipping and Virginia Company-associated networks; search for English records after migration dates that could falsify candidate identities.

# FamilySearch Center / Archive Hit List

The purpose of this file is to turn broad research questions into exact books/pages or record groups that can be opened quickly at a FamilySearch Center or archive.

## Surry County, Virginia
### Immediate target A
**Surry County Deeds No. 5 (1694–1709), p. 348**
Expected event: John Rawlings of Southwark Parish → Robert Partin of same; 200 acres north side Blackwater Swamp.
Capture:
- image number / film;
- full page plus facing page;
- preceding and following entries;
- witnesses;
- consideration;
- acreage/bounds;
- acknowledgment date;
- wife's relinquishment if any;
- clerk/recording notation.

### Immediate target B
Search deed/order-book indexes for Robert Partin, Charles Partin, John Partin, Henry Partin, Parton, Partyn, Partain from 1700–1760. Do not rely only on P indexes: inspect variant surname sections.

## Halifax County, North Carolina
### Immediate target C
**11 Aug 1767 deed**, William and Ann Hux → John Partin, reportedly 100 acres adjoining Great Darlling Swamp.
Capture complete deed, witnesses, bounds, adjoining owners and recording proof.

### Immediate target D
**1795 John Partin will/probate**
Capture will image and every surviving estate paper, inventory, sale, account, bond and distribution. Do not stop at an abstract.

## Virginia colonial records

1624/25 muster — Robert Partin household, West & Sherley Hundred, 22 Jan 1624/5.

### Immediate target F
General Court proceedings concerning Margaret Partin / Thomas Hale, 1626–27. Capture exact spelling, charge, testimony, sentence, relationship language, and any statement naming Thomas's father/occupation.

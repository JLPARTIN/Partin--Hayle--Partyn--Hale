# Canonical Record Note — John Rawlings to Robert Partin, 1706

## Locator
Surry County Deeds No. 5 (1694–1709), p.348. Recorded 7 May 1706, per derivative abstract.

## Reported transaction
John Rawlings of Southwark Parish to Robert Partin of the same parish; **200 acres on the north side of Blackwater Swamp**, described as land taken up by the grantor's father by patent dated 29 Apr 1692.

## Evidence status
**B+/STRONG DERIVATIVE — original image required.**

## Forensic importance
This is the only exact 1706 Partin deed citation surfaced on the Rawlings compilation. The same webpage contains contradictory narrative substitutions of Charles and Robert; the exact citation itself names **Robert**. A separate Charles 1706 deed must not be presumed to exist without an index/original hit.

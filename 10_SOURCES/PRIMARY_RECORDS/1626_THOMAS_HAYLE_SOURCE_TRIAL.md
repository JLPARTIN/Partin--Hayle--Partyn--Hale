# Source Trial — Thomas Hayle, General Court, 4 June 1626

## Public transcription
A public transcription presents the court heading as 4 June 1626 and gives Thomas Hayle as age 21, son of Symon Hayle of St Mary Somerset in London, porter.

## Independent secondary corroboration
An excerpt from Martha W. McCartney's biographical dictionary independently summarizes Thomas Hale/Hayle as son of Symon Hayle of St Mary Somerset Parish in London, a porter, and reports his October 1623 arrival aboard the *George*.

## Dependency caution
These are not necessarily independent witnesses. McCartney and the web transcription may both derive from the same colonial court entry. Count them as corroboration of a source tradition, not two independent primary records.

## Chronological test
If the 19 May 1605 St Mary Somerset baptism candidate is correct, Thomas would be 21 on 4 June 1626 — exactly the age reported by the court transcription.

# Canonical Record Note — Justice/Cooper Adjacency, 1754–1755

## 1754
John and Henry Person → Richard Wiggins Jr., 213 acres; adjoining William Cooper, John Justice, John Nicholson and Benjamin Ricker. Surry Deed Book 7, pp.128–130, per independent compiled citation.

## 1755
Richard Wiggins Jr. and Tabitha → Michael Carsley, same 213-acre neighborhood; adjoining William Cooper, John Justice, Benjamin Rickes/Ricker and John Nicholson. Surry Deed Book 7, pp.187–188.

## Significance
Two later transactions independently preserve William Cooper and John Justice as adjoining landowners. Cooper acquired a large tract from Gregory Rawlings in 1724; Justice acquired 200 Blackwater acres from Robert Partin in 1727.

## What it proves
It supports a stable neighborhood relationship between the grantees of Gregory Rawlings and Robert Partin.

## What it does not prove
It does not alone establish that Robert's 1727 tract was the exact 1706 Rawlings-derived 200 acres.

# Canonical Record Note — William Partin, Halifax, 1796

## Reported transaction
14 Feb 1796 — William Partin acquired 80 acres on the south side of Hunter's Mill Swamp.

## Boundary language
Recovered research reports the tract as beginning in/on **John Parten's line** and running by a branch from/near **John Partine Plantation**.

## Significance
The deed physically embeds William in a John Partin land neighborhood immediately after the elder John's 1795 death.

## Identity problem
Which John does the boundary preserve?
- the deceased elder John's former plantation;
- John B before/after migration;
- another John?

The wording and title history must decide.

## Priority
Recover the original deed, grantor, consideration, full metes/bounds and prior title.

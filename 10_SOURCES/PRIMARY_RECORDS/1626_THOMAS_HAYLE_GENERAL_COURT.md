# Canonical Record Note — Thomas Hayle General Court Indictment

## Record
Virginia General Court proceeding, reported as 4 June 1626 in a secondary transcription.

## Reported wording / facts
A published web transcription gives Thomas Hayle as **age 21**, “the sone of Symon Hayle” of **St Mary Somerset, London**, and gives Symon's occupation as **porter**. Thomas is reported convicted on four indictments for rape of four children and sentenced to death.

## Evidence status
**STRONG DERIVATIVE — original/authoritative printed court text not yet checked by this repository.**

This is materially stronger than saying Virginia merely “connects” Thomas to a Symon of London: if faithful, the court itself states father, parish, and occupation.

## Critical contradiction
The recovered London will transcription identifies a Symon Hayle of St Mary Somerset as a **tailor**, while this court transcription calls Thomas's father Symon a **porter**. Do not merge those occupational descriptions by assumption.

## Date problem
The secondary transcription dates the indictment 4 June 1626, while the Margaret Partin concealment proceeding is in 1627 and calls Hayle “lately executed.” The exact sequence must be checked against the authoritative court edition/manuscript.

## What this can prove if verified
- Thomas Hayle's father was named Symon Hayle.
- Symon was associated with St Mary Somerset, London.
- Thomas was about 21 at indictment.
- The stated occupation of Symon was porter.

## What it does not prove
- That the Symon in the recovered 1625/26 will is the same Symon.
- That Margaret Partin was a Hayle.
- That Thomas was kin to Robert or Margaret Partin beyond his documented household service.

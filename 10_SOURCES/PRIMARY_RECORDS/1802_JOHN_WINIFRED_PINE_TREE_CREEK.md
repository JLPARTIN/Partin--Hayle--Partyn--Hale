# Canonical Record Note — John & Winifred Partin, Kershaw, 1802

## Date
24 Feb 1802 conveyance; Winifred dower acknowledgment reported 4 Mar 1802.

## Property
150 acres on Pine Tree Creek, Kershaw District, South Carolina.

## Wife
Winifred/Winefred relinquished dower before J.P. William Lang/Langley.

## Reported boundaries
John Burchett and Joseph Alexander.

## Source locator
Recovered project notes point to Kershaw deed volumes D–F, film 24045 / DGS 7649006.

## Reading conflicts
Recovered notes disagree over:
- grantee surname: De Bruhl vs Drake;
- witness: M. Vaughan / Waughan vs related reading;
- magistrate rendering Lang vs Langley.

These are paleographic/source-control problems, not reasons to choose whichever reading fits a theory.

## Significance
Repeated John + Winifred pairing with formal dower acknowledgment provides the strongest bridge from the 1793 Halifax couple to the Kershaw couple.

# Canonical Record Note — Gregory & Hannah Rawlings to William Cooper, 1724

## Locator
Surry County Deeds, Wills, Etc., Book 7 Part 2, pp.588–589. Dated 15–16 Sep 1724 in an independent land-reconstruction compilation.

## Reported transaction
Gregory and Hannah Rawlings → William Cooper; 489 acres adjoining Richard Washington and Richard Shockey.

## Evidentiary significance
John Rawlings's 1702/03 will names Gregory as brother of Eliza Partin. The 1724 land therefore gives a documented sibling-family geographic anchor.

## Later convergence
1754 and 1755 deeds place William Cooper and John Justice as adjoining owners. John Justice is Robert Partin's 1727 grantee.

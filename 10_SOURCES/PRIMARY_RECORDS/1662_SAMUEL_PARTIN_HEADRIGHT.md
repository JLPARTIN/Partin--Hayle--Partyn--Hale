# Canonical Record Note — Samuel Partin Headright, 1662

## Reported patent
11 Mar 1662 — Dennis Eyes and Dennis Conway, 270 acres in Northumberland County on the north side of the main branch of Great Wiccocomoco River, for transportation of six persons including **Samll. Partin**.

## Source convergence
The entry appears in a compiled Partin research PDF and independently in a separate transcription of the patent.

## Importance
This is direct evidence that a Partin-named person outside the known Robert Sr./Jr. Shirley Hundred context appears in Virginia's patent system by 1662.

## Caution
It does not establish Samuel's residence, origin, kinship or actual 1662 immigration date.

# Canonical Record Note — Symon Hayle Will, St Mary Somerset

## Record
Recovered transcription of a will dated 10 Feb 1625/26 for **Symon Hayle, tailor, St Mary Somerset, London**.

## Reported family
- wife Elizabeth, residuary legatee/executrix
- son Thomas, £100 at age 21
- son Robert
- daughter Anne
- brother Thomas Hayle
- sister Anne Fisher
- sister Joane [surname uncertain]
- cousin Symon [surname uncertain]

If son Thomas died before 21, his £100 was reportedly divided between Robert and Anne.

## Evidence status
**STRONG RECOVERED TRANSCRIPTION — original Guildhall/LMA probate record not yet independently inspected.**

## Correct interpretation of the missing Margaret
The will does **not** name a daughter Margaret. That is evidence against a claim that Margaret Partin was a surviving **daughter of this Symon**. It does **not** test whether Margaret was Symon's sister, cousin, or member of another Hayle branch. The earlier repository-wide rejection of all Hayle identity hypotheses on this basis was therefore too broad.

## High-value identity test
The Virginia court transcription reportedly calls Thomas Hayle age 21, son of Symon of St Mary Somerset. This will names a son Thomas who had not yet reached 21 when the will was made. That is a close chronological and familial fit, but the occupation contradiction (porter vs tailor) must be resolved before the two Symons are merged.

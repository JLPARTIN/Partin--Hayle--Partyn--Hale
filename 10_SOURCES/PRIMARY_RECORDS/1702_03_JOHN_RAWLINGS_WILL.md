# Canonical Record Note — John Rawlings Will, Surry County

## Locator
Surry County Will Book 5, p.279. Made Aug 1702; proved 25 May 1703, per published/digitized abstract.

## Reported dispositive family
- son John Rawlings — land at Blackwater
- son Gregory Rawlings — home plantation, 126 acres, tools/books
- **daughter Eliza. Partin** — livestock/property already in her possession
- wife Mary — residue and executrix
- witnesses Robert Nicholson, Hannah Nicholson, Sampson Lainer

## Evidence status
**B+/STRONG DERIVATIVE.** The abstract is independently recoverable in a digitized Surry wills/administrations compilation. Original Book 5 p.279 remains the A-level target.

## What it proves at current level
A contemporary will abstract identifies an Eliza/Elizabeth as John Rawlings's daughter and already bearing the Partin surname by Aug 1702. It also makes Gregory her brother and establishes the Blackwater land context.

## What it does not prove
It does not name Eliza's husband. It therefore does not by itself prove Robert Partin or Charles Partin was her husband.

## Identity consequence
This record must be read with the 1706 John Rawlings→Robert Partin deed and the 1727 Robert + wife Elizabeth transaction, but those records remain separate propositions until the same Elizabeth is explicitly bridged.

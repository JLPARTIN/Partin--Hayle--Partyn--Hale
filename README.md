# Partin–Hayle / Partyn–Hale Forensic Genealogy

## Thames to the James — and Beyond

This repository is the central research archive for the Partin / Parton / Partyn and Hayle / Hale families: their English origins, the early Virginia/Jamestown generation, the Surry–Sussex–Halifax descendants, later southern migrations, and Y-DNA evidence.

The project is intentionally **evidence-first**. It preserves proven facts, candidate identifications, rejected claims, unresolved bridges, and research targets separately rather than turning plausible reconstructions into pedigree facts.

## Research rules

1. **Primary record before pedigree.**
2. Preserve the spelling used by each source: Partin / Parton / Partyn / Partain; Hayle / Hale / Hales, etc.
3. Distinguish:
   - **PROVEN** — direct or converging documentary evidence establishes the proposition.
   - **STRONG** — substantial evidence, but a decisive link is still missing.
   - **WORKING HYPOTHESIS** — plausible and testable.
   - **CANDIDATE** — record/person worth investigating; identity not established.
   - **REJECTED / UNSUPPORTED** — evidence contradicts it or no adequate evidence supports it.
4. DNA is corroborative evidence, not a substitute for documentary parentage.
5. Every dossier should distinguish transcription, interpretation, and inference.
6. Negative searches matter and should be logged.
7. Never silently merge same-name people.

## Current high-value questions

The central English question is whether the London records identify the immigrant Robert Partin/Partyn and explain his relationship to the Hayle family. The central Virginia question is how the early Jamestown-era family connects, if at all, to the later documented Surry/Sussex Partin population. The central downstream question is the exact documentary path from Virginia into Halifax County, North Carolina and the later Carolinas–Mississippi line.

This repository is a living forensic case file. Claims will be upgraded or downgraded as original records are recovered and compared.

## Research recovery (September 2026)

A systematic recovery of the project owner's scattered AI-assisted research — accumulated across dozens of chat sessions and saved to Google Drive between January and April 2026, plus one externally authored book-length secondary source — was consolidated into this repository. Start here:

- `00_PROJECT_CONTROL/RECOVERED_RESEARCH_INVENTORY.md` — full catalog of every source recovered, deduplicated, with reliability notes.
- `00_PROJECT_CONTROL/MASTER_CLAIM_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md` — the sixteen central propositions of the project, each graded against the recovered evidence.
- `00_PROJECT_CONTROL/REJECTED_AND_QUARANTINED_CLAIMS.md` — claims tested and found unsupported (a Hayle maiden name for Margaret Partin; a Hewitt maiden name for John Partin's wife; the 1746 Surry payment as a "smoking gun"; and others).
- `08_SOURCES/SOURCE_CONTRADICTIONS/MASTER_CONTRADICTION_REGISTER.md` — 23 specific, unresolved factual contradictions preserved rather than silently harmonized.
- `00_PROJECT_CONTROL/RESEARCH_RECOVERY_LOG.md` — what was and was not covered by this recovery pass.

**The single highest-value discovery of this recovery pass** is a verbatim 1627 Virginia General Court record naming Thomas Hayle's victim as Avis Partin, "the daughter of Robert Partin and Margarett his wife" (`02_JAMESTOWN_VIRGINIA/GENERAL_COURT_1627.md`), alongside a recovered 1625/26 London will for Symon Hayle that — read together with the court record — actively rejects, rather than confirms, this project's long-standing working hypothesis that Margaret Partin was born a Hayle (`01_ENGLAND_THAMES/LONDON_NETWORK.md`).

**The second-highest-value discovery** is the identification of *Barnabas Partin: Ancestors and Descendants, From Virginia to Mississippi and Beyond*, a book-length compiled genealogy by the project owner's cousin Jim (Jimmy Dail) Partin, previously scattered across ~30 duplicate scans and OCR passes in the project's Drive under two different naming schemes. It is now catalogued as a single canonical source (`08_SOURCES/MASTER_SOURCE_REGISTER.md`) and integrated into `04_HALIFAX_NORTH_CAROLINA/` and `05_CAROLINAS_TO_MISSISSIPPI/`.
